# Corrections and Open Questions

This file exists so the project does not silently erase earlier mistakes or turn uncertainty into false certainty.

## Confirmed correction: Iris

### Earlier assumption
The originating retrospective initially discussed **Iris** as though she might have been a naturally eccentric real caller whose exact degree of performance was uncertain.

### Correction
Iris was a **fictional recurring caller voiced by Ron Bennington**.

### Consequence
All future files should treat Iris as part of the deliberately constructed fictional caller universe, not the real-caller branch.

This correction also strengthens a larger historical point: early *Ron & Fez* already mixed fictional callers and real callers in the same continuity, long before the later XM work/shoot ambiguity became central.

## B.L. — major genealogical correction/extension

Earlier R&F-focused discussion risked treating B.L. primarily as a later recurring caller/friend.

New source review shows B.L. / **Brenda Lee** belongs much earlier in the genealogy:

- current SportsChix biography says she began in the late 1980s as a part-time Ron & Ron call screener and daily on-air talent;
- she co-hosted **Hooters on the Radio**, created by Ron Bennington;
- surviving 1996 audio metadata places Brenda Lee/B.L., Julie Williams, Lynne Austin, Ron Diaz, Ron Bennington and Fez together;
- later she co-founded SportsChix and continued appearing on *Bennington*;
- in 2024 she launched *Those Florida Days with B.L.*, making her a potentially valuable oral-history source.

Consequence: BL should be treated as a **Florida prehistory / multi-decade continuity figure**, not merely an XM civilian caller.

The exact BL/Fez romantic/sexual lore remains unresolved until original audio is reconstructed.

## Ron's role

Avoid two opposite simplifications:

### Over-orchestration myth
“Ron secretly planned every breakdown.”

No evidence supports that as a general model.

### Passive-observer myth
“Ron just happened to be there.”

Also inadequate. Ron demonstrably recognized structures, prolonged conversations, selected pressure points, paired personalities, and converted real events into radio.

Best current model:

**Ron orchestrated the frame more often than the underlying event.**
