# Source-quality hierarchy for community research

Prefer, in order:

1. original full-show audio/video;
2. surviving RonFez.net/Paltalk/Wackbag pages from the period;
3. publisher-generated XM episode summaries;
4. contemporaneous detailed fan logs;
5. later firsthand participant oral history;
6. later community recollection;
7. tertiary character wikis/summary sites.

When sources conflict, preserve both versions and identify the conflict rather than forcing resolution.
