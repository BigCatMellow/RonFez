# Terminology and Running-Joke Dictionary

This is a deliberately expandable glossary. Definitions are intentionally more detailed than a normal wiki because many R&F references function as compressed lore.

## AFRO Show

A crossover format involving **Anthony, Fez, Ron, Opie** — hence A-F-R-O. Associated with the WNEW/O&A relationship and broader shared radio ecosystem.

## All Secret Society / Big ASS

The show's deliberately ridiculous fan-membership identity.

**Big ASS** = Big All Secret Society / All Secret Society wordplay associated with the membership card system.

Listeners could have **Big ASS Card numbers**, identify themselves by number when calling, and participate in sponsor/contest rituals.

The exact joke wording and formal expansion should be verified from original material, but the social function is clear: a physical badge of membership in the R&F community.

## Big ASS Card

Physical membership card/number identifying a listener as part of the All Secret Society.

Historical importance:

- on-air caller identification;
- fan status marker;
- connection between radio and physical community;
- still referenced years later at retirement/memorial events.

## Big ASS Prize Closet

On-air reward destination for callers who made a strong contribution, especially in formats such as Comedy Pyramid.

The prize itself might be a promotional CD/DVD/box set or miscellaneous station item. The true status reward was **making Ron laugh**.

## Big ASS Bash

Major 2007 Hard Rock Cafe event associated with East Side Dave, the Midnight Rider storyline, professional wrestlers, staff, and fans.

Needs full event case file.

## Big ASS Night of Fights

Live fight-event tradition involving staff/fans and wrestling-style storylines.

The originating conversation specifically connected Al Dukes/Rory Hamptons material with this history.

Do not automatically equate with the user's remembered phrase “Friday Night Fights” until researched.

## Big ASS Room

Fan-created **Paltalk** room for Ron & Fez listeners.

Important because it provided synchronous fan interaction and sometimes webcam access, functioning almost as a parallel audience room.

## Board Gossip

East Side Dave segment built from online fan discussion.

Typical loop:

`show → board argument → Dave reports argument on-air → Ron comments → board reacts → next Board Gossip`

The segment made the fan community itself a recurring source of show content.

## budday / budday girl / cousin budday

R&F community greeting/identity language strongly associated with Fez and the broader fan world.

Still used immediately by returning listeners during Fez memorial discussion years after the show ended.

Exact origin and first usage need dedicated research.

## Cakehorn

Late-era word used by Fez for a mouth, becoming one of the most famous final-period R&F terms.

### Known
Ron immediately recognized the comic potential and expanded the phrase into a prolonged linguistic interrogation.

### Disputed
The first origin may have been:

- genuine verbal mistake;
- handwriting/misreading issue;
- confusion with “cakehole”;
- prearranged work.

Current status: **origin unresolved; escalation deliberately worked/improvised.**

Associated later vocabulary includes **tootin'** and “shut your cakehorn”-type references.

## Comedy Pyramid

Early recurring caller format in which Ron supplied a topic/premise and listeners called with short jokes or associations, each adding another “block.”

Historical importance:

- distributed writers' room;
- caller training system;
- way for listeners to earn Prize Closet access;
- concentrated example of Ron's improvisational/editorial style.

## Cookie

Command/phrase associated with Fez's cat **Skippy** in late-era material.

The humor arose partly from Fez's insistence that Skippy could perform/respond and the cat's failure to cooperate convincingly in studio.

Needs exact clip/timestamp catalog.

## Davepound

Named East Side Dave fan faction, especially visible during Dave/Fez rivalry and the 2010 Save Dave campaign.

Counterpart/rival identity: **Whatley Posse**.

## D-Man

Late Fez character/device presenting a tougher, more confident persona.

Historically useful because it sometimes appeared to give Fez access to older character-performance energy when ordinary conversation was difficult.

## Fezetorial

Structured Fez commentary/editorial format particularly associated with earlier R&F.

Important corrective to late “Silent Fez” memory because it preserves Fez actively driving prepared/structured comedy.

## Friday Night Lights

Recurring Fairfax-era Friday social gatherings involving Fez and listeners/friends at a bar called **Thursdays**.

Content cycle:

`Friday social event → conflicting stories → Ron investigates Monday → board/callers contribute`

Final Friday Night Lights: **July 29, 2005**, same date as final WJFK show.

## “Friday Night Fights”

Phrase remembered by the user as part of the event culture.

Current project status: **unresolved terminology**.

Do not silently substitute Big ASS Night of Fights. Research whether this was a distinct event, alternate name, or memory crossover.

## Fez Jr.

Nickname associated with **Intern Popcorn**, reflecting his late-era entanglement with Fez and similarities/parallels the show drew between them.

## Inner Voice Fez

Late-era theatrical device in which Fez personified his extremely self-critical internal dialogue and Ron effectively interviewed or interacted with that “voice.”

Do not misuse the term as evidence of psychosis; the show was theatricalizing self-talk/negative internal commentary.

## I Shall Be Released

Song strongly associated with the final 2005 WJFK period and listener interpretation that Ron was using music choices to hint at the impending move to XM.

Needs exact play-date chronology.

## Nature Boy

Fez wrestling/persona reference associated with Ric Flair-style performance and later fan vocabulary.

Needs exact character chronology and distinction from other wrestling bits.

## North American Scum

LCD Soundsystem track associated with later XM-era opening identity/audio production discussed in the retrospective.

Mooch Cassidy and Chris Stanley are linked to elaborate show-opening montages; exact history should be reconstructed by broadcast date.

## Paul O, your life's a movie

Long-running framing of Paul O's bizarre personal continuity.

The phrase became especially recursive when Paul actually made the film ***Gap***.

## Paltalk

Live chat/webcam service used by the R&F fan community.

Historically important because it created real-time audience interaction alongside the broadcast, unlike asynchronous message boards.

## RonFez.net

Major fan-community site that functioned as:

- discussion board;
- social network;
- archive;
- event photo repository;
- soundboard/song repository;
- rumor system;
- source material for radio;
- later memorial archive.

The restored 2023 version became the **Fez Marie Whatley Memorial RonFez.Net Media Archives**.

## Save Dave

2010 fan campaign supporting East Side Dave during his SiriusXM pay/workload dispute.

Used Twitter-era hashtags including variants such as:

- `#SaveDave`
- `#SaveESD`
- `#DavePound`

Important as evidence of the fan community migrating from centralized boards toward social media organizing.

## Satellite of Love

Lou Reed song deeply associated with the show's satellite-era identity, closing tradition, final 2015 farewell, and later Fez memorial emotion.

Should receive full music-history case file.

## Siren Series

Recurring Dave-versus-Fez competition/trivia structure, including subjects such as Batman and *Star Wars*.

Comic game on the surface; professional-rivalry subtext underneath.

## Skippy

Fez's cat, central to late-era segments in which Fez claimed the cat could respond/perform certain behaviors.

The comedy grew from the cat's failure to demonstrate the promised behavior while Fez continued defending the claim.

Associated commands/phrases include **Cookie** and “Skippy kiss.”

## stand-alone fruit

Late-era Fez phrase/reference that remained sufficiently memorable to function as fan shorthand years later, including FezCon-era community language.

Needs dedicated origin clip and explanation before a definitive definition is written.

## Steakgate

Late-era conflict built around Fez promising steaks and producing Steak 'n Shake steakburgers.

Current forensic classification:

**likely WORK → genuine conflict/SHOOT → return to WORK.**

The hamburger/steak semantic argument eventually exposed deeper Fez/Chris workplace frustration.

## The Plan

Recurring Dave/Fez-era idea that Dave was maneuvering to replace Fez as Ron's co-host.

### Supported
- Dave's airtime and importance increased;
- Fez appeared threatened;
- Ron used the tension;
- fans chose sides.

### Not currently established
- literal secret Ron/SiriusXM/Dave agreement to remove Fez.

Current forensic status: **literal plan unproven / likely storyline; underlying insecurity plausible.**

## Whatley Posse

Named Fez fan faction, especially visible in contrast with the Davepound during late Dave-era rivalry.

## work / shoot

Borrowed from professional wrestling terminology.

### Work
Constructed/performed premise presented as real.

### Shoot
Genuine event or emotion.

R&F often requires mixed classifications such as:

- WORK → SHOOT;
- SHOOT → WORK;
- collaborative exaggeration;
- real event turned into a bit;
- unresolved origin with worked escalation.

## Glossary expansion rule

Any phrase that would make a returning listener say **“I remember that, but I have no idea where it came from”** belongs here.

No term is too minor. Future entries should include:

- first known use;
- exact date;
- speaker;
- original context;
- evolution;
- later callbacks;
- whether it crossed into message-board vocabulary;
- surviving clip/source.
