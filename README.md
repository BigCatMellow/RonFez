# Ron & Fez — Retrospective, Archive, and Forensic Reference

This repository organizes a long-form retrospective and forensic reconstruction of **The Ron & Fez Show** and the unusually large social world around it.

The goal is not merely to summarize the program. It is to preserve and investigate:

- the full chronology from the *Ron & Ron* roots through WNEW, WJFK/Fairfax, XM/SiriusXM, the final years, and the post-show legacy;
- recurring bits, games, characters, music, events, and production traditions;
- the RonFez.net / Paltalk / live-event fan ecosystem and the way listeners became participants, contributors, characters, friends, archivists, and historians;
- the relationships among hosts, producers, interns, callers, friends of the show, and fans;
- the distinction between documented fact, participant recollection, contemporary fan record, later fan consensus, and unresolved lore;
- famous work/shoot disputes and places where genuine events, performance, improvisation, and real emotion became inseparable;
- an eventual essential-listening map that lets a new or returning listener experience the evolution of the show in context.

## Governing rule

**No detail is too small.**

Obscure callers, forgotten board handles, one-off event details, deep-track music choices, rejoiners, production quirks, tiny phrases, contradictory recollections, fan-created songs, intern arcs, photo captions, and uncertain lore all belong here when they can be sourced or clearly labeled as uncertain.

The intended direction is **more detail, not compression**.

## Evidence policy

- **A — Direct:** original broadcast, contemporaneous participant statement, official documentation
- **B — Strong corroboration:** multiple independent contemporary accounts or strong later firsthand corroboration
- **C — Participant recollection:** later account by Ron, Fez, Dave, Earl, Chris, etc.; valuable but subject to memory and performance
- **D — Contemporary fan record:** RonFez.net, forum posts, listening logs, event posts from the period
- **E — Later fan consensus:** Reddit, YouTube comments, oral lore; useful lead, not proof
- **U — Unresolved:** evidence conflicts or does not allow a confident conclusion

The project explicitly preserves uncertainty rather than converting fan lore into fact.

## Important correction already captured

The originating retrospective initially treated **Iris** as though she might have been a naturally eccentric real caller. The forensic pass corrected this: Iris was a **fictional recurring caller voiced by Ron Bennington**. That correction is preserved explicitly rather than silently overwritten.

The next deep pass is the **off-air / civilian-government forensic reconstruction**:

1. map RonFez.net administration, forums, major handles, and schisms;
2. reconstruct the Paltalk Big ASS Room and its major personalities;
3. map RonFez.net / Wackbag / Paltalk relationships;
4. trace specific Board Gossip items back to original fan posts where possible;
5. identify incidents that began at parties, bars, Friday Night Lights, or Paltalk and later became on-air storylines;
6. turn the first cast genealogy into individual person files;
7. expand the work/shoot ledger into evidence packets;
8. build the full event calendar, music database, and essential-listening map.

Nothing here should be treated as finished. This repository is intended to become a detailed historical reference, not a polished short article.
