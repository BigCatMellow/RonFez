# Tuddle — Patrick Fowler

## Classification

**Former Ron & Fez producer / Central Florida radio stunt personality and producer / cross-show continuity figure / later R&F caller/guest**

## Public identity

Modern professional and podcast records identify the longtime radio personality simply as **Tuddle**. A current LinkedIn result identifies him as **Patrick “Tuddle” F.** in the Orlando radio/media world, while older radio-reference material gives the full name **Patrick Fowler** and identifies him as a former *Ron & Fez* producer.

Sources:
- https://www.linkedin.com/in/tuddle
- https://en-academic.com/dic.nsf/enwiki/1429593
- https://en-academic.com/dic.nsf/enwiki/11762604

An unrelated but official 2015 Florida legal filing independently identifies a radio figure **Patrick Fowler (a/k/a “Tuddle”)**, strongly corroborating the name/alias equivalence.

Source:
- Florida litigation exhibit surfaced by Bay News 9 / NY1 search archives, identifying Patrick Fowler as “Tuddle.”

Evidence grade: **A/B for alias/name equivalence; B/D for early R&F employment role.**

## Orlando / Monsters transition

After R&F, Tuddle became part of the **Monsters in the Midday / Monsters in the Morning** world at Real Radio 104.1 in Orlando.

Later reference material describes him as:

- former Monsters intern;
- former *Ron & Fez* producer;
- producer/personality on multiple Central Florida shows;
- one of the Monsters' recurring stunt participants.

Sources:
- https://en-academic.com/dic.nsf/enwiki/11762604

## July 15–16, 2002 — coffin/concrete stunt

RFNet preserves a dedicated audio package:

> **July 15 & 16, 2002 — Ron & Fez call Tuddle in a Coffin**

The archive description says Tuddle agreed to spend **48 hours in a coffin encased in concrete** as a *Monsters in the Midday* charity stunt, and that Ron & Fez repeatedly called him while he was inside to freak him out.

Source:
- https://www.ronfez.net/forums/downloads.php?do=cat&id=60

This is a particularly useful genealogy case because Tuddle had already left R&F, but his next show's stunt was still treated as **R&F continuity**.

The relationship did not end at employment termination.

## Career longevity

Tuddle's public podcast identity emphasizes more than twenty years of broadcasting experience. A 2020 post under the Tuddle identity describes himself as a radio journeyman and explicitly names R&F among the major shows he worked on.

Source:

## Work/shoot issue

Tuddle's later stunt-heavy career raises an important forensic point.

The fact that Central Florida radio used Tuddle as a stunt personality does **not** automatically mean every stunt was wholly spontaneous or wholly real. Contemporary radio culture often used worked or embellished premises.

For the 2002 coffin stunt, however, the narrow historical claims are strong:

- a coffin/concrete charity stunt occurred;
- Tuddle was the participant;
- R&F called him during it;
- Tuddle himself later remembered it as his buried-alive-in-concrete bit.

The exact safety setup, physical conditions, charity and degree of theatricalization remain open.

## Core interpretation

Tuddle is a good example of **staff genealogy rather than cast turnover**.

He left the R&F payroll, but not the story. His new radio life in Orlando remained reachable enough that Ron and Fez could call into a bizarre charity stunt and instantly fold their former producer back into R&F continuity.
