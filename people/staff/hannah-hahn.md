# Hannah Hahn / Hanna Han — early WNEW-era figure

## Classification

**Early WNEW/Dot Com-era producer or staff figure / occasional on-air participant / spelling-disambiguation problem / living-oral-history lead through Tasteless Ginny**

## Name problem

The surviving record currently contains two spellings:

- **Hannah Hahn** — used by RonFez.net's audio archive;
- **Hanna Han** — the spelling Tasteless Ginny used in a 2023 firsthand post when identifying an old WNEW photograph.

Until a contemporary staff document resolves the spelling, this archive should **not silently choose one and erase the other**.

The filename uses `hannah-hahn.md` because that spelling appears in the surviving RFNet audio catalogue, but both forms remain indexed.

The phrase “in honor of the return of Hannah Hahn” implies that, when the file was uploaded to RFNet in 2007, Hannah had **reappeared in the contemporary R&F universe** strongly enough to prompt resurrection of old audio.

The exact 2007 return date/appearance remains to be located.

## Producer versus character problem

An old deleted-Wikipedia character list places **Hannah Han** among WNEW-era R&F characters.

Source:
- https://wikibin.org/articles/list-of-ron-and-fez-show-characters.html

That is not strong enough to define her formal job.

Ginny's direct 2023 recollection instead identifies Hanna as a **producer**.

These can both be true in R&F terms: producers routinely became on-air characters.

Current safest formulation:

> **Hanna/Hannah was an early WNEW-era producer/staff figure who also appeared on-air enough to become part of the show's character memory.**

Exact title and tenure remain open.

## Core interpretation

Hanna/Hannah is a useful reminder that the early R&F staff record is incomplete enough that **even the spelling of a producer's name can be unstable twenty-five years later**. The correct response is not to guess; it is to preserve both source forms and build outward from the surviving audio and participant testimony.
