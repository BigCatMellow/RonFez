## Research principle

For every person, ask not only **“who was this?”** but:

> **How did this person enter the R&F system, what relationships made them useful, who shaped the radio identity, and how much was consciously performed?**

Modern participant testimony is tracked in [`../reference/08-living-oral-history-watch.md`](../reference/08-living-oral-history-watch.md). Governance/staff-source provenance is also indexed in [`../reference/09-governance-staff-and-simulcast-source-index.md`](../reference/09-governance-staff-and-simulcast-source-index.md).

### Detail and privacy policy

No figure is excluded merely for being obscure. But modern people-search/data-broker services are not used to expose real identities behind old handles. Handle-only biographies are acceptable and often more historically faithful.
