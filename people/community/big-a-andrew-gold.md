# Big A — Andrew Gold

## Classification

**Shared O&A/R&F satellite-era personality / fan-media figure / photographer/podcaster / recurring Fez relationship character**

## Why Big A matters

Big A is a particularly useful case for understanding the difference between the **O&A and R&F uses of the same civilian personality**.

He moved through the shared XM/Sirius ecosystem, but later firsthand oral history specifically identifies his **relationship with Fez** and says the two shows used him differently.

That makes Big A less a “borrowed O&A character” than a cross-show person whose meaning changed depending on the host structure around him.

## Core interpretation

Big A shows why the R&F universe cannot be mapped as a closed cast. The satellite ecosystem had **shared civilians**, but Ron's distinctive contribution was often turning the shared person into a relationship with history.

With Big A, the relationship that mattered most was Fez.
