# Death Metal Moe

## Classification

**Early/long-running RFNet civilian personality / Big ASS Card holder / metal musician / physical-community-era figure**

## Why Death Metal Moe matters

Death Metal Moe is a good example of how a name that survives as an occasional old-show reference can represent a much larger off-air identity.

The currently recoverable evidence connects him to:

- the old RFNet social generation remembered around the **first 2002 Christmas party**;
- years of ordinary RFNet participation;
- a formally numbered **Big A.S.S. card**;
- a death-metal band called **Unhallowed**;
- long-running personal continuity with other founding-era personalities such as Matty Fridays.

The result is not yet a complete biography, but it is enough to move Death Metal Moe out of the generic `community figures still queued` list.

## Big A.S.S. card holder #9170

A November 19, 2009 post on **DroidForums.net** is particularly valuable because the user identifies himself through R&F-specific credentials on a site outside RFNet.

Posting as **Death Metal Moe**, he says he wants an XM app so he can stream Ron and Fez on his new Droid and signs the post with:

> **Big A.S.S. card holder 9170 checkin' in.**

Source:
- https://www.droidforums.net/threads/sirius-xm-satelite-radio-app.1378/

**Evidence grade: B/D — external contemporary post with explicit self-link to R&F identity.**

This establishes:

- Death Metal Moe was still listening in 2009;
- the handle was used beyond RonFez.net;
- **Big A.S.S. Card #9170** was publicly claimed by that handle.

The card number should therefore be preserved as part of his R&F identity.

## Music: Unhallowed

A March–April 2021 Reddit thread asks where to find music from:

> **Unhallowed — Death Metal Moe's band**

On April 5, an account responds:

> `Hey there. I actually am Death Metal Moe.`

The writer says **Matty Fridays** alerted him that someone was looking for the band's music and offers to provide the group's **whole catalogue**.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/m5qhew

### What this establishes

This is strong first-person-style continuity evidence for:

- Death Metal Moe having played in a band called **Unhallowed**;
- a catalogue substantial enough to be described as the band's whole catalogue rather than one novelty recording;
- continuing direct contact with **Matty Fridays** roughly two decades after the founding RFNet period.

**Evidence grade: C/B — later self-identification in the old R&F community, corroborated by the context of the request.**

### What is not yet established

- Death Metal Moe's real/public name;
- instrument/role in Unhallowed;
- band lineup;
- active years;
- hometown;
- releases/titles;
- whether Unhallowed ever performed at an R&F event;
- whether any band material was played on-air;
- whether unrelated bands named `Unhallowed` found in metal databases are the same group.

There are multiple bands using the name **Unhallowed**. Do **not** merge an external metal listing into this biography without a direct Death Metal Moe/R&F bridge.

The 2021 music-recovery response contains a tiny but important social fact:

## Core interpretation

Death Metal Moe was not simply a metal-themed caller nickname.

The surviving record shows a durable RFNet identity who remained in the community for years, publicly carried **Big A.S.S. Card #9170**, made music with **Unhallowed**, and was still connected to founding-generation RFNet people such as Matty Fridays two decades later.

He is another piece of the larger pattern this archive keeps uncovering: the R&F civilian cast was simultaneously **radio material, internet community, physical social network and creative scene**.
