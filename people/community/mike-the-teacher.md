# Mike the Teacher

## Classification

**Fan/community photographer / event documentarian / source of photographs later curated by Mikeyboy / WJFK-XM social-world figure**

## Why Mike the Teacher matters

Mike the Teacher is a good example of a person whose historical importance is easy to miss if the archive only counts microphone time.

The surviving RonFez.net gallery repeatedly credits him as the **source/photographer of major event images**, while Mikeyboy appears as the account that uploaded/published those images.

That lets us distinguish two archival roles:

- **photographer / original documentarian** — Mike the Teacher;
- **site uploader / archive custodian** — often Mikeyboy.

This division of labor is a small but important clue about how fan-created media reached RFNet.

## Big ASS Bash photographs

A surviving gallery item is explicitly titled:

> **Big Ass Bash Pics from Mike the Teacher**

The page lists **mikeyboy** as the uploader and is dated May 27, 2007.

Source:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2308/size/external.php/ppuser/27045

The May gallery date is months after the February 23 Big ASS Bash, showing that archival material could enter the site well after the event itself.

That is another methodological warning:

> **RFNet gallery date ≠ event date unless independently established.**

## Dave and Casey wedding photography

Multiple surviving Dave/Casey wedding gallery pages are labeled/described as:

> **Wedding Pictures by Mike the Teacher**

This makes Mike a recurring event photographer rather than the source of one stray image packet.

The wedding is already one of the best-documented cases where a staff member's private life, show storyline, formal event and fan social world collapse together. Mike's photographs are part of the reason the physical event can still be reconstructed.

## Archival chain

The evidence suggests a workflow roughly like:

`MIKE TAKES PHOTOS → PHOTOS REACH MIKEYBOY/RFNET → MIKEYBOY UPLOADS → COMMUNITY COMMENTS/IDENTIFIES PEOPLE → IMAGES SURVIVE AS ARCHIVE`

This should be tested against additional galleries rather than assumed universally.

## WJFK-era lead

A 2021 listener recalling the final WJFK/Fairfax period specifically remembers **Mike the Teacher** among otherwise obscure names and details. This is E-level memory but suggests his importance may predate the 2007 photographs.

Research source lead:
- retrospective thread on the final WJFK show / Friday Night Lights.

## “Teacher” label

Do not infer school, employer, subject taught, or legal identity from the nickname alone. Unless Mike publicly established those details, the archive should retain **Mike the Teacher** as the historical identity.

## Bronx Johnny Sextravaganza wedding artifact

A Dave/Casey wedding gallery image is titled **Bronx Johnny's Sextravaganza**, with BronxJohnny appearing in comments. Since Mike the Teacher is repeatedly credited as photographer for the wedding series, this may place him behind a visual artifact tied to Bronx Johnny's recurring on-air segment/persona.

This needs exact page-level confirmation before attributing that individual photograph to Mike.

## Why fan photographers mattered

R&F's community history is unusually visual because fans photographed:

- weddings;
- fight nights;
- bar nights;
- holiday parties;
- staff interactions;
- ordinary regulars.

Station publicity photography would naturally center Ron, Fez and celebrities.

Fan photography centers **the social network**.

That difference is why obscure people such as Snoogans, Leticia, Hottub, GVac, Irish Alkey and others remain recoverable today.

## Core interpretation

Mike the Teacher is part of the **unpaid documentation layer** that made the R&F civilization durable. His importance is not that he necessarily said the funniest thing on-air; it is that he helped create the visual record by which we can identify who was actually there.
