## A revealing production anecdote from a WJFK worker

A 2026 Reddit participant who says they **worked at WJFK** remembers Cigar Sid screening calls in the back room. According to the recollection:

1. Sid instant-messaged Ron with a comment related to the show topic.
2. Ron told Sid to wait roughly **30 minutes** before coming into the studio and saying it on-air.
3. Sid waited, entered, and delivered the remark.
4. Fez then criticized Sid for not staying on-topic.
5. Fez reportedly continued correcting him during the break.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/1saof8j/what_were_some_rf_moments_that_hit_you_hard/

**Evidence grade: C — later claimed firsthand workplace recollection.**

This anecdote is not verified by the original broadcast, but if accurate it is unusually revealing about Ron's production instincts: **delay a relevant remark until it appears strangely mistimed, then let Fez's genuine-seeming frustration become the comedy.**

That is an excellent candidate for a future work/shoot micro-case.
