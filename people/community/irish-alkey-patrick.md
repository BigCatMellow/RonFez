# Irish Alkey — Patrick

## Classification

**RonFez.net / friends-of-show community member / physical-event participant / memorialized member of the fan network**

## Public identity

RonFez.net's June 2011 funeral-donation announcement identifies the community member as:

> **Patrick — Irish Alkey**

A fuller surname has not yet been established from a source strong enough to include.

## Physical community evidence

A surviving RonFez.net gallery image dated December 14, 2008 is captioned:

> **Silera, Crazed, GAZ, Alkey and Reilly**

This confirms that Alkey was not merely a screen name appearing in threads; he participated in the real-world social scene with other recognizable community members.

Source:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/4671/ppuser/8357

## Death

RonFez.net's community notice says Patrick died suddenly on the morning of **Tuesday, June 14, 2011**.

The next day the site asked listeners/members to help defray funeral expenses.

The wording matters:

> “our community has been saddened”

That phrase is direct evidence of how site members understood themselves by 2011.

Source:
- https://www.ronfez.net/forums/printthread.php?t=90775

The same notice appears in the surviving front-page news archive:
- https://www.ronfez.net/forums/showthread.php?t=7946

## Why Irish Alkey matters

Irish Alkey is not historically important because of one gigantic famous radio bit—at least none has yet been identified.

He matters because his death shows the fan community functioning as a **real social support institution**.

By 2011, the network could:

- recognize a member's death collectively;
- notify a dispersed community;
- organize financial support;
- preserve the person's identity in event photographs and later memory.

That is stronger evidence of community reality than any claim that “the boards felt like family.”
