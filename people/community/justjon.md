# JustJon

## Classification

**Founding-era RonFez.net member / early media uploader / front-page community organizer / likely leadership figure**

## Account chronology

Surviving gallery pages show JustJon registered in **February 2001**, placing the account at the site's founding period.

A surviving gallery snapshot shows approximately **24,017 posts**.

## Early archive work

JustJon is credited as uploader of several very early RonFez.net media items, including:

- **Lizzy Grubman — Bowling For White Trash photo pack**, added July 20, 2001;
- **BILLY STAPLES BANGED HIS ASS UP**, added May 23, 2002.

The Lizzy Grubman item's description jokes about “56k'ers,” a useful period detail showing the technical environment in which fans were sharing media.

The Billy item is also historically revealing because its description editorializes about whether Billy really fell down stairs, showing that the archive could preserve **fan theory alongside media**.

## Community/event leadership

By 2010–11, JustJon is posting front-page event/news material including:

- RonFez.net Christmas Party 2010;
- tenth-anniversary announcement;
- tenth-anniversary party at Idle Hands on February 19, 2011.

The tenth-anniversary language explicitly celebrates:

- friendships found;
- members lost;
- good times had.

That makes JustJon a useful window into how site leadership understood the community after a decade.
