# J-Dubs — Justin Johnson

## Classification

**WJFK-era R&F producer/participant / physical-stunt character / co-host of The Hideout / later multi-market programmer / 98 Rock Baltimore program director**

## Identity — triangulated

The current **Hideout Radio Archives** identifies J-Dubs as one of the show's two hosts and links his current social account as **@dubsradio**.

A 2025 professional profile of **Justin Johnson**, newly named Program Director of Baltimore's 98 Rock/WIYY, identifies his Instagram as **@dubsradio** and documents a career path including WJFK in Washington and WTKS in Orlando—the same markets associated with *The Hideout*.

Together, these independent sources make the identification very strong:

> **J-Dubs = Justin Johnson**

Sources:
- https://www.thehideoutradioarchives.com/
- https://barrettmedia.com/2025/08/22/justin-johnson-drives-98-rocks-swagger-and-service/

**Evidence grade: A/B triangulated public professional identity.**

## Early radio career

The 2025 Barrett Media profile says Johnson became fascinated with radio while young, attended **Specs Howard School of Broadcast Arts** after graduating high school in Fowlerville, Michigan, in 1999, and began his career at **WWDX in Lansing, Michigan**.

It then places him in programming/on-air roles across:

- WJFK — Washington, D.C.
- WTKS — Orlando
- WWBA — Tampa
- WKLS — Atlanta
- WEBN — Cincinnati
- WIYY/98 Rock — Baltimore

Source:
- https://barrettmedia.com/2025/08/22/justin-johnson-drives-98-rocks-swagger-and-service/

This professional chronology independently matches the city sequence remembered by *The Hideout* archive.

## Why J-Dubs is structurally important

J-Dubs' early R&F identity appears unusually physical.

Across several events he becomes the person who can be:

- fed hot peppers;
- used as a golf target;
- paired in pain/endurance challenges;
- put into a boxing match.

That suggests the show recognized a reusable trait: **willingness to participate physically**.

But unlike many friends of the show, J-Dubs then converts that visibility into a substantial independent radio career.

# The Hideout

The current **Hideout Radio Archives** says the show:

- was hosted by **El Jefe and J-Dubs**;
- ran in one form or another from **2003 to 2008**;
- originated at **106.7 WJFK in Washington**;
- later moved to **Real Radio 104.1 / WTKS in Orlando**;
- also appeared via XM and in markets including Atlanta, New York and Washington again;
- began as a **late-night Saturday show**;
- later became a weeknight program;
- also had weekend-afternoon version **NEXT!** and internet podcast **NEXTcast**.

Source:
- https://www.thehideoutradioarchives.com/

**Evidence grade: A/B — dedicated current archival project for the show.**

## The Heretics — a daughter fan culture

The Hideout archive explicitly says the show's fans were called:

> **The Heretics**

and that they:

- created fan boards;
- fought **MySpace battles**;
- generated new audio and video content;
- interacted heavily with the program.

This is extremely important to R&F history.

The R&F/WJFK environment appears to have helped produce not merely new talent but a **new show with its own participatory fan civilization** using many of the same mechanisms:

`RADIO → BOARD/FAN COMMUNITY → FAN-CREATED MEDIA → RADIO`

That makes *The Hideout* a kind of daughter culture of the Fairfax R&F ecosystem, although it should be researched on its own terms rather than treated as an R&F clone.

---

## Hurricane Isabel connection

A U.S. congressional record says that after R&F devoted their Hurricane Isabel broadcast to listener emergency reports, WJFK replaced normal syndicated programming with a special edition of **El Jefe's Hideout** to continue local coverage.

Source:
- https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-108hhrg92537/pdf/CHRG-108hhrg92537.pdf

This gives The Hideout an unusually serious role very early in its life: it participated in the station's local emergency-information response, not just comedy programming.

Whether J-Dubs was already formally co-hosting that exact special or joined shortly afterward should be checked against the 2003 Hideout archive rather than assumed.

## Later career

Radio Ink reported in August 2025 that Justin Johnson would become **Program Director of 98 Rock/WIYY Baltimore**, effective September 1, after serving as Assistant Program Director since 2023.

It also says he previously served as Program Director at **WEBN Cincinnati from 2019–2023** and worked on iHeart's national rock-format branding.

Source:
- https://radioink.com/2025/08/18/baltimores-98-rock-names-justin-johnson-program-director/

This gives J-Dubs one of the clearest conventional radio-career trajectories of any WJFK-era R&F supporting personality.

---

## Why the trajectory matters

J-Dubs' path is almost the inverse of Earl's.

### Earl
professional radio employee → personal behavior becomes comedy character.

### J-Dubs
radio employee/participant → physical comedy character → independent co-host → programmer/management career.

Both demonstrate that being turned into R&F material did not necessarily mean being trapped as only a “character.”

## Core interpretation

J-Dubs is one of the clearest cases where the Fairfax R&F laboratory **produced another broadcaster rather than merely another character**. His later career also makes *The Hideout* a crucial branch of this forensic map: R&F's participatory culture appears to have propagated into a separate show whose own fans built boards, media and lore.
