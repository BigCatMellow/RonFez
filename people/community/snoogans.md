# Snoogans

## Classification

**High-volume RonFez.net member / 2007 physical-event participant / long-term general-community user / example of a persistent screen-name identity**

## Why Snoogans matters

Snoogans is a useful test case for the proposition that not every historically significant community figure needed much radio airtime.

The surviving RFNet record shows a username with:

- years of continuity;
- tens of thousands of posts;
- physical-event presence;
- ordinary non-radio discussion long after the peak friends-of-show era.

That is exactly the infrastructure by which a name could carry enormous meaning to insiders while sounding meaningless to a casual listener.

## Core interpretation

Snoogans demonstrates why the R&F secondary universe became opaque to outsiders. A single screen name could represent **ten years and tens of thousands of accumulated interactions plus real-world meetings**. The host did not need to explain that history every time; the community already carried it.
