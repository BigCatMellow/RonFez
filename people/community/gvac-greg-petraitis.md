# GVac — Greg Petraitis

## Classification

**Community figure / caller / RonFez.net founding-era member / later Weird Medicine participant**

## Why GVac matters

GVac is one of the strongest documented examples of the Ron & Fez fan pipeline becoming a durable identity beyond the original program.

He was not merely a recurring caller. Surviving evidence places him in:

- the earliest RonFez.net period;
- high-volume board participation;
- physical R&F gatherings;
- recurring calls recognized warmly by Ron;
- long-term friendships originating in the community;
- later involvement with Doctor Steve / *Weird Medicine*;
- a large memorial network after his death.

## Founding-era status

FoundryMusicJeff Shain's memorial entry says he and GVac were part of the early days of RonFez.net, which he remembers as the **first message board for the radio show**.

Another early member, spoon, remembers first joking with GVac on rf.net around **2000/01**.

The site's own anniversary dates RonFez.net to approximately February 5, 2001, so treat “2000/01” as approximate participant memory rather than evidence for a 2000 launch.

## Offline social life

Spoon's memorial entry is unusually detailed. It remembers:

- roughly sixteen years of knowing GVac;
- 2001 bar crawls;
- hockey-team arguments;
- a “massive softball comeback”;
- many R&F events;
- extended board humor/jabs involving Matty and JustJon;
- continued phone friendship after geography separated them.

This is direct participant evidence that early board relationships became real-world friendships almost immediately.

## Radio identity

A memorial contributor named Marge recalls GVac as a familiar R&F caller and remembers Ron greeting him as **“our friend GVac.”**

This is retrospective memory rather than original audio, but it fits GVac's broader documented status in the community.

## Weird Medicine

Multiple memorial entries explicitly connect GVac to **Weird Medicine** with Doctor Steve. This gives him an unusually complete fan-to-media trajectory.

## Death and memorial

GVac died in 2016. Doctor Steve's memorial guestbook accumulated **171 entries**.

The comments are sociologically valuable because they range from:

- people who knew him personally;
- early board members;
- radio colleagues;
- listeners who never met him but nevertheless regarded him as a familiar friend.

One commenter explicitly reflects on radio's ability to make a person feel like a friend even when the listener has never seen them.

## Core interpretation

GVac is important because he shows that “friend of the show” could become a **long-lived social/media identity** rather than a temporary radio gag.

His path was approximately:

`listener → early rf.net member → offline friend network → recurring caller → recognized friend → Weird Medicine participant → memorialized community figure`

That arc spans the entire lifecycle of the R&F secondary universe.
