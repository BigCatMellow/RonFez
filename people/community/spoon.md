# spoon

## Classification

**Founding-era RonFez.net member / very high-volume poster / physical-community participant / oral-history witness / late-board feud figure in fan memory**

## Why spoon matters

Spoon is valuable for two separate reasons.

First, surviving RFNet metadata shows a remarkably long-lived, high-volume account.

Second, spoon's 2016 memorial entry for GVac contains one of the richest surviving firsthand descriptions of **what the earliest RonFez.net community actually did together off-air**.

That makes spoon not just a subject but a potentially important **witness** for reconstructing the social world.

## GVac memorial: early-community oral history

In the 2016 memorial guestbook for **Greg Petraitis / GVac**, spoon writes a long personal remembrance covering roughly sixteen years of friendship.

The entry remembers:

- first jokes on rf.net around **2000/01**;
- **2001 bar crawls**;
- arguments over hockey teams;
- a memorable softball comeback;
- many R&F-related events;
- ongoing jokes and clashes involving **Matty and JustJon**;
- continued friendship by phone after geographic separation.

Source:
- https://www.doctorsteve.com/gvac-memorial/

**Evidence grade: C — direct participant recollection written in memorial context.**

### Why the “2000/01” wording matters

RFNet's own tenth-anniversary announcement dates the site's institutional launch to approximately **February 5, 2001**.

Spoon's looser “2000/01” memory therefore should not be treated as proof the board formally existed in 2000.

It may indicate:

- precursor discussion/community;
- memory rounding after fifteen years;
- a soft-launch period.

The discrepancy is preserved rather than “corrected” away.

---

## 2001 bar crawls

Spoon's memorial recollection is currently among the strongest evidence that RFNet users were **meeting physically during the board's first year**.

This pushes the online→offline social pipeline very early:

`new message board → repeated handles → bar crawls within months`

The exact crawl dates, bars and attendee lists remain to be recovered.

### Research targets

- RFNet event threads from 2001;
- gallery/photos if any survive;
- GVac/JustJon/Matty references;
- Ron/Fez on-air mentions;
- whether these were official Big ASS events or civilian-organized nights.

## Softball

Spoon specifically remembers a **“massive softball comeback.”**

The exact game/date is unclear.

RFNet galleries independently prove that organized **RF Softball** existed later, including an August 18, 2007 event.

This does not prove spoon was referring to the 2007 game; given the memorial chronology, the remembered comeback could be an earlier game.

The softball history therefore deserves a separate event chronology.

Source for later softball artifact:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/3091/ppuser/37907

## JustJon and Matty

Spoon's memorial entry remembers years of joking/attacking involving **Matty and JustJon**.

Later 2022 fan recollections of RFNet decline specifically remember **Matty Fridays and Spoon** as dominating shrinking listening threads with insults/conflict.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/tuozzu

**Evidence grade: C/E.**

### Identity caution

Surviving RFNet gallery pages also contain a user/person referred to simply as **Friday**.

It is not yet established that:

> Friday = Matty Fridays

Therefore this archive keeps them separate until direct evidence links the identities.

## Persistence into the late board

Searchable original RFNet material shows spoon still active in later-year discussions, including **2013**.

That gives the account more than a decade of visible continuity from the 2001 registration era.

This is important because the late-board conflicts remembered by others were not necessarily strangers entering an old community. They involved people carrying **ten-plus years of accumulated relationships**.

---

## Community role versus formal role

Spoon's massive post count and long tenure do not establish moderator/admin status.

The user signature visible in one gallery page includes:

> `~Mod Tested, Admin Approved!~`

That reads like a joke/status signature, not a formal permissions record.

Do not interpret it as evidence that spoon was a moderator.

## Why the late feud matters structurally

If later memories are accurate that listening threads shrank while recurring personal conflict among old users became more visible, spoon/Matty is a useful case for a general problem of mature online communities:

> **shared history increases intimacy, but also increases accumulated grievance.**

The exact argument cannot yet be reconstructed, so the project should not assign blame.

The historically interesting question is how a community that had once generated jokes, events and friendships changed when some of those same relationships became repetitive conflict.

## Core interpretation

Spoon is exactly the kind of person a normal radio history would omit and a serious R&F history cannot.

The account represents **more than a decade of accumulated board identity**, and spoon's own memorial testimony preserves details—2001 bar crawls, softball, long friendships—that help demonstrate how quickly a radio message board became a real social network.
