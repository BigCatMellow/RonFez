# Perrynoid

## Classification

**Fan-created radio persona / caller / musician / WJFK-era friend-of-show / fan-production contributor**

## Why Perrynoid matters

Perrynoid is one of the clearest cases where a listener did not merely become a character because Ron found them odd. The person behind Perrynoid later described himself as having **“played the role” of Perrynoid**, implying conscious construction/performance layered over genuine community membership.

This places Perrynoid between two familiar categories:

- not a wholly fictional caller like Iris;
- not simply an employee whose natural behavior became a character like Earl.

Instead:

> **a real fan deliberately built a persona that the show could use.**

Source lead:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/nl53rj/

## Era

Perrynoid is especially associated with the **WJFK/Fairfax period**, when local listeners could move unusually easily between calling, visiting, events and physical social life around the show.

## Music / fan production

Perrynoid is closely associated in fan memory with **“Fix the Phones”** and related musical responses to the show's chronic technical problems.

The exact title/version history must be verified because listeners sometimes use several phrasings for the phone song.

What matters structurally is clear:

`show has technical problem → fan turns problem into song → show plays song → song becomes recurring show vocabulary`

That is the secondary universe functioning as an **external creative department**.

## Cherrynoid

The name **Cherrynoid** appears in Fairfax-era historical lists and is clearly relationally paired with Perrynoid in the show's naming/lore.

Do not infer the exact personal relationship solely from the names. This needs direct audio/community evidence.

## Persona question

The phrase “played the role” raises several forensic questions:

- Was “Perrynoid” substantially different from the person's ordinary personality?
- Did Ron/Fez help shape the persona?
- Were calls prepared in advance?
- Were songs self-initiated or requested?
- Did listeners understand Perrynoid as a character at the time?

This is exactly the kind of gray zone that makes the R&F cast genealogy interesting.

## Core interpretation

Perrynoid is an important counterexample to the idea that Ron alone “made characters” out of civilians.

Some fans understood the medium and **arrived already willing to perform themselves**.

That means the show eventually had a sophisticated audience capable of producing not merely calls but recurring personas and finished creative assets.
