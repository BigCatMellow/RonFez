# Don Stugots

## Classification

**High-volume RonFez.net member / event photographer-commenter / physical-community participant / host of the Don Stugots Experience BBQ / later memorial-community voice**

## Why Don Stugots matters

Don Stugots is a good example of a fan identity that became large enough to leave traces in several different layers of the R&F world:

- RFNet profile metadata;
- photo uploads and comments;
- named social events;
- apparent podcast/secondary-media activity;
- later memorial participation.

He is therefore useful for reconstructing the **middle tier between board poster and recurring media personality**.

## RonFez.net profile

Surviving gallery pages show:

- username: **Don Stugots**
- registered: **December 2005**
- location: **Brooklyn, NY**
- posts: **28,742** in the surviving snapshot
- custom title including **“Patron Saint of the Innernets.”**

Another self-deprecating custom-title line mixes Brooklyn/Italian/Jewish identity for comic effect. It is preserved as a period profile artifact, not treated as a literal ethnic-biographical statement beyond what the user chose to write publicly.

Sources:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2815/ppuser/38051
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2809/ppuser/16872

## Big ASS Bash photo archive

Don's member gallery contains photographs tagged/captioned around the **Big ASS Bash**, including:

## Winter Carnival participation

Don appears in comments on the 2007 Winter Carnival gallery and directly identifies people in photographs, including **CT & Jivin**.

Another photo caption names:

> **The Don, Leticia ... and Cockstrong**

with Don himself commenting on the image.

Sources:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/3361/size/big/cat/500
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/3343/ppuser/38850

This is strong evidence that Don was not simply a remote poster. He was physically embedded in the party/social scene and knew other members well enough to correct/identify gallery metadata.

## The Don Stugots Experience BBQ

RonFez.net has a dedicated gallery category spelled:

> **The Don Stugots Experiene BBQ**

The misspelling `Experiene` is preserved because it appears in the site's breadcrumb/category text and may reflect the original category name rather than a search-engine error.

Hottub-uploaded photos in that category are dated **June 11, 2007**.

Sources:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2565/ppuser/44981
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2562/ppuser/18105

The photo date does not automatically establish the exact BBQ date. It does establish that the event/category existed and that Hottub documented it.

### Why the BBQ matters

A fan/community personality apparently had an event important enough to receive its own RFNet gallery category. That shows the social network could generate events **not centered solely on Ron and Fez themselves**.

At that point the fan universe has acquired its own event calendar.

## Night of a Hundred Podcasts lead

Later fan memory references a **Night of a Hundred Podcasts** around February 2008 and associates Don Stugots/secondary shows with that ecosystem.

This remains E-level until original promo/audio/event listings are recovered.

Research should determine whether `The Don Stugots Experience` was:

- a podcast;
- internet-radio show;
- recurring RFNet program;
- event-only joke name;
- some combination.

## Bobo song lead

Later fans remember a Bobo song with wording like **“Give Don Stugots a Shot.”** This is currently E-level oral-history material.

If recovered, it would be another instance of one civilian character creating/receiving material about another civilian character.
