# Mikeyboy

## Classification

**RonFez.net founding-era member / former administrator / photographer / archivist / occasional show participant**

## Why Mikeyboy matters

If RonFez.net functioned as the show's external memory, Mikeyboy appears to have been one of the people physically maintaining that memory.

His surviving account is attached to a remarkable portion of the photographic record of the R&F social world.

## RonFez.net account

Surviving gallery pages repeatedly show:

- username: **mikeyboy**
- registration: **December 2001**
- location: Manhattan
- posts: **33,389**
- long-running user title: **“MojoPin put it there back in the day, and now it's huge.”**

The title is preserved because it is clearly meaningful internal lore; its exact origin is not yet established.

## Administrator status

In a 2021 old-school rf.net roll-call thread, the account says:

> “mikeyboy, former admin. So many memories”

Treat this as direct participant testimony that Mikeyboy served as a RonFez.net administrator.

## Episode-log/history association

Later fans credit Mikeyboy with major show-log/chronology work. A 2021 discussion around old episode guides contains direct interaction with the person identifying as Mikeyboy, strengthening the association, but a complete authorship/version history has not yet been reconstructed.

## Studio/show participation

Later listeners remember Mikeyboy being in studio and participating in quiz/Siren Series-related material. These claims need full-show audio confirmation.

## Archival significance

The most important distinction is that Mikeyboy did not merely preserve **the hosts**.

His gallery preserved:

- fans;
- bars;
- weddings;
- parties;
- staff interactions;
- odd artifacts;
- gatherings that otherwise might now exist only as references in audio.

This is why the gallery can be treated almost as an ethnographic record of the R&F community.

## Core interpretation

Mikeyboy represents **fan as institutional memory**:

`listener → board member → admin → event participant → photographer → archive custodian → retrospective historian`

Without this class of participant, the R&F universe would survive mainly as disembodied audio. Mikeyboy's contribution helps us see what that world physically looked like.
