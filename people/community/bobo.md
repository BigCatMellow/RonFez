# Bobo

## Classification

**WJFK-era fan/community musician / physical-event regular / later XM friends-of-show figure / obscure but persistent civilian-cast node**

## Why Bobo matters

Bobo is one of the people whose importance becomes clearer only when the archive is read sideways rather than chronologically. A song credit survives in the WJFK file library, then the same name appears physically in the later XM-era Red Rock West social cluster, and Dave's later Big ASS Bash oral history again lists Bobo among the people around the ticket controversy.

That gives a multi-era trace:

`fan creator → community personality → physical event attendee → XM-era continuity`

## March 11, 2005 — Dan Rather song

The surviving RonFez.net file library contains a highlights entry for **March 11, 2005** that explicitly lists:

- **Bobo's Dan Rather song**;
- worst places to wait;
- Perrynoid's Crazy Jen song;
- Perrynoid's birthday.

Source:
- https://ronfez.net/forums/downloads.php?do=cat&id=42&sort=date

**Evidence grade: A/D — surviving RFNet archive metadata.**

This places Bobo inside the Fairfax-era fan-music economy at the same time Perrynoid was actively writing songs about community personalities.

## Why the song credit matters

Fan songs were more than novelty recordings. They could convert a listener into a recognizable creator because the host could replay the song, use it as a transition, or associate the person's name with a recurring situation.

Bobo's surviving credit therefore represents a **production contribution**, not merely fandom.

## Red Rock West

A 2007 RFNet gallery caption from **Red Rock West Bar Night** names a group containing:

- Snoogans
- Mikeyboy
- Faith
- **Bobo**
- Kevin Ogre
- Smiler Grogan

This supplies direct physical-event evidence that the Bobo identity remained embedded in the community after the move to XM.

The exact Red Rock West event date remains under investigation.

## Big ASS Bash / ticket controversy

Dave McDonald's later *Tales From the Satellite* episode about Earl and the Big ASS Bash ticket controversy explicitly lists **Bobo** among the personalities involved in the surrounding story, alongside Al Dukes, Don Wickland, Mikeyboy and Hard Rock Johnny.

Source:
- https://sites.libsyn.com/471843/episode-xlvii-earl-the-ticket-controversy

**Evidence grade: C — participant retrospective.**

This is additional evidence that Bobo had crossed from WJFK fan-musician identity into the core XM social/event network.

## “Give Don Stugots a Shot” lead

Later fan memory attributes another song to Bobo with a refrain/title remembered approximately as **“Give Don Stugots a Shot.”**

This remains E-level until the audio/file is recovered. It is preserved because, if confirmed, it would demonstrate a recurring R&F community mechanism:

> one civilian fan character creating music about another civilian fan character.

That is the community producing its own internal folklore.

## Name ambiguity

“Bobo” is a common nickname and exists elsewhere in talk-radio culture. Do not merge this R&F Bobo with unrelated O&A/Howard/other radio figures unless direct evidence establishes equivalence.
