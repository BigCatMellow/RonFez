# Matty Fridays

## Classification

**Founding/early RonFez.net-era civilian personality / 2002 live-event participant / later symbol of long-running RFNet interpersonal conflict**

## Why Matty Fridays matters

Matty Fridays is easy to misclassify as merely a late-era argumentative message-board poster because that is how some later fans remember the name.

A much earlier event source changes that picture:

> **Matty Fridays fought GVac for the RonFez.net Championship at Big ASS Night of Fights II on May 17, 2002.**

That establishes Matty as a recognizable enough RFNet personality to be formally cast in a major R&F live event only about fifteen months after the site's working February 2001 launch date.

Source:
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IK9RtC_Xtxs

**Evidence grade: B/D archival event recording/description.**

---

## Big ASS Night of Fights II — May 17, 2002

The surviving timestamp map places the **GVac vs. Matty Fridays** match at approximately **02:55:26** into the commercial-free event recording.

The billed stake is:

> **the RonFez.net Championship**

This is significant beyond the people involved. By May 2002 the board had developed enough internal social identity that a mock championship between two message-board personalities could be presented to a live R&F audience without extensive explanation.

See:
- `../../events/2002-05-17-big-ass-night-of-fights-ii.md`

## Relationship to GVac

The match proves a public/event connection between Matty and **GVac / Greg Petraitis** by 2002.

GVac's 2016 memorial contains spoon's recollection of years of early RFNet jokes and antagonism involving **Matty and JustJon**.

That does not establish that Matty and GVac were genuine enemies. In a wrestling-styled R&F event, board rivalry could be friendly performance, real irritation, or both.

Source:
- https://www.doctorsteve.com/gvac-memorial/

**Evidence grade: C for later participant memory.**

---

## Relationship to spoon

A 2022 retrospective discussion of RFNet's decline remembers **Matty Fridays and Spoon** as recurring antagonists in late/shrinking listening threads.

One participant remembers daily listening threads shrinking dramatically and says much of the remaining activity became Matty and Spoon insulting one another.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/tuozzu

**Evidence grade: C/E.**

This is a useful lead but does not establish:

- who initiated the feud;
- whether it was serious;
- exact dates;
- whether it crossed into Board Gossip;
- whether the two were friends offline;
- whether the fighting was partly performative.

## 2022 residual reputation

Another 2022 old-RFNet discussion immediately jokes that what a former member remembers most is hostility toward **Matty Fridays**.

The fact that the name can still produce an instant reaction roughly two decades later is evidence of **durable board reputation**, not proof that the negative reputation was objectively deserved.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/y3dwpj

## “Friday” identity problem

Surviving RFNet gallery pages also contain a person/user referred to simply as **Friday**, including:

- a Bar 9 image captioned **Reilly and Friday**;
- a later gallery comment listing `Funkman, Stugots, Friday, Earlshog`.

At present there is **not enough evidence to establish that “Friday” and “Matty Fridays” are the same person**.

They therefore remain separate archive identities.

This is exactly the sort of alias assumption that can corrupt a genealogy if later fan shorthand is treated carelessly.

## Core interpretation

The May 2002 RFNet Championship is enough to establish that Matty Fridays belongs to the **founding-generation civilian cast**, not merely the board's decline phase.

The later hostility attached to his name may have been the accumulated result of years of community history. The next task is to reconstruct those years rather than letting the ending define the whole biography.
