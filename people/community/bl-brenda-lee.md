# B.L. — Brenda Lee

## Classification

**Pre-R&F Ron & Ron-era personality / call screener / Hooters on the Radio co-host / later SportsChix host / recurring Ron & Fez and Bennington personality**

## Why B.L. matters

B.L. is not merely a satellite-era caller or “friend of the show.” She belongs to the **prehistory of Ron & Fez** and is one of the clearest surviving examples of a person whose relationship with Ron and Fez spans multiple radio systems and decades.

Her continuity runs roughly:

`Ron & Ron → Hooters on the Radio → SportsChix → Ron & Fez appearances → Bennington cameos → post-R&F Florida-media retrospectives`

That makes her a genealogical bridge between Ron's Florida radio world and the later R&F universe.

Current 102.5 The Bone / SportsChix material identifies B.L. as **BL** and describes her career in first-person/official-show-biography terms.

A historical 1996 *Hooters on the Radio* recording identifies her as **Brenda Lee (aka B.L.)**.

## Ron & Ron entry

The SportsChix bio says BL began her radio career in the **late 1980s** as:

- a part-time call screener;
- daily on-air talent for the nationally syndicated **Ron & Ron Radio Network**.

This is significantly earlier than the R&F show and means BL knew Fez/Todd Hillier from the Florida ecosystem before he became Ron Bennington's named co-host.

**Evidence grade: B/C — current professional biography.**

---

## Hooters on the Radio

The same bio says BL became co-host of **Hooters on the Radio**, created by Ron Bennington.

A surviving 1996 audio listing independently names:

- Brenda Lee / B.L.;
- Julie Williams;
- Lynne Austin;
- Fez Whatley;
- Ron Diaz;
- Ron Bennington.

The February 22, 1996 recording is especially useful because it preserves the shared continuity directly: Ron and Ron have the Hooters show cast in studio, and Fez is part of the conversation.

Source:
- https://music.amazon.in/podcasts/cc82e207-a422-472b-971e-40abe1224904/episodes/ecacbd6c-ee4d-4b85-9db1-4f6afe592744/south-florida-radio-archives-ron-ron-show---hooters-on-the-radio-february-22-1996

A 2013 autograph collector also independently remembers attending a *Hooters on the Radio* live gig at a Tampa Hooters and identifies the signed promotional photo as featuring **Julie Williams, Lynne Austin, B.L. and Fez Whatley**.

## SportsChix

The current SportsChix biography says BL later co-founded and hosted **The SportsChix** on CBS Radio Tampa in the late 1990s.

It credits the group with:

- multiple Super Bowl Radio Row appearances;
- interviews with major sports figures;
- later ESPN exposure.

The important R&F point is that BL did not remain merely a supporting Ron character. She developed an **independent broadcasting career** and then continued re-entering Ron's later shows.

Source:
- https://www.theboneonline.com/fp/sportschix/?outputType=amp

## Fez relationship / influence

The SportsChix bio explicitly says:

> the late Fez Whatley was an inspiration throughout BL's career.

This is one of the few current professional sources directly acknowledging the durable BL–Fez connection.

However, the enormous body of R&F joking about their relationship—including sexual stories, feuds and insults—needs a dedicated audio chronology before individual jokes are treated as literal biography.

### Forensic rule

Do not use later fan paraphrases such as “BL screwed Fez” as fact without original audio/context. The relationship is unquestionably longstanding; the exact truth content of every famous story is not.

## Return to Bennington

The SportsChix bio lists **2014–present cameo appearances on Bennington SiriusXM**.

That makes BL one of the longest surviving recurring personalities in Ron Bennington's radio continuity.

## Post-R&F oral history

In 2024 BL launched the limited podcast **Those Florida Days with B.L.**, explicitly centered on 1980s Tampa Bay, Hooters, Ron & Ron and the era's personalities.

This makes BL a potentially valuable **primary oral-history source** for the earliest Ron/Fez world.

Source:
- https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/those-florida-days-with-b-l/id1770196117

## Core interpretation

BL is not a side character who happened to call *Ron & Fez*.

She is a **living piece of Ron and Fez's Florida prehistory** who repeatedly re-enters the story. Understanding BL properly requires starting before *The Ron & Fez Show* existed.
