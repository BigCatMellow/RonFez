# Moshin

## Classification

**Early RFNet physical-community figure / 2002 first-Christmas-party participant by later labeled-photo identification / subject of the unresolved high-download artifact `Moshin's Tattoo`**

## Why Moshin matters

Moshin is exactly the kind of person who disappears from a conventional Ron & Fez history.

The current evidence is sparse, but three separate archive surfaces preserve the name:

1. RFNet's surviving File Library still lists **`Moshin's Tattoo`** among its most-downloaded files;
2. a fan-compiled R&F civilian/cast census created in late 2007 includes **Moshin** among recognizable show/message-board figures;
3. a 2023 discussion of a **labeled photograph from the first RFNet X-Mas party** identifies **Moshin** among the people visible in the group image, which participants identify as **2002**.

Together those are enough to preserve Moshin as a real early-community identity even though the tattoo artifact and full biography remain unrecovered.

## `Moshin's Tattoo`

The restored RFNet File Library's global **Most Popular Files** block includes:

> **Moshin's Tattoo**

with roughly **9,800+ accumulated downloads** in current crawl snapshots.

Sources:
- https://www.ronfez.net/forums/downloads.php?do=cat&id=60
- https://ronfez.net/forums/downloads.php?do=cat&id=49

The file's prominence is remarkable because the title assumes users already knew who Moshin was and why the tattoo mattered.

### What the title proves

Very little beyond the existence of an artifact important enough to be archived under Moshin's name.

It does **not** establish:

- tattoo design or wording;
- that it was an R&F/Big ASS logo;
- whether it was permanent;
- when it was done;
- where it was done;
- whether it happened at a live event;
- whether the tattoo itself was sincere, comic or part of a stunt.

Do not fill any of those blanks from inference.

The direct File Library item ID/detail page remains unrecovered.

## 2007 fan census — useful but previously misread

A preserved deleted-Wikipedia page created December 30, 2007 lists **Moshin** in a large fan-compiled census of Ron & Fez producers, contributors, listeners and message-board figures.

Source:
- https://wikibin.org/articles/list-of-ron-and-fez-show-characters.html

### Correction to earlier archive notes

An earlier research pass incorrectly described Moshin as appearing in a discrete **`WNEW Era`** section of that page.

The surviving page actually places Moshin in the broader section titled:

> **`Current Era: XM, WFNY-FM, and messageboard posters`**

That distinction matters.

The page therefore supports only:

> **By late 2007, fans still recognized Moshin as part of the broader R&F/message-board civilian universe.**

It does **not** independently date his first appearance to WNEW.

The early chronology instead comes from the later identification of Moshin in the 2002 first-RFNet-Christmas-party photograph.

### Source quality

The Wikipedia article was deleted specifically because it was unreferenced and failed notability standards.

Treat it as:

> **low-grade fan documentation of remembered names, not an authoritative biography.**

## Core interpretation

Moshin remains mysterious, but no longer invisible.

The strongest current reconstruction is simple:

> **Moshin was a recognizable member of the early RFNet physical community by the 2002 first Christmas-party generation, remained memorable enough to appear in a 2007 fan census, and had a tattoo-related artifact so prominent that it remains among RFNet's most-downloaded surviving files.**

The tattoo itself is still the missing center of the story. Recovering that one record could explain why Moshin became memorable enough that a generic title needed no further introduction.
