The surviving show history says the night included:

- a screening of **Caddyshack**;

## The event is built as a movie-world translation

Unlike a generic screening, the surviving description suggests R&F translated recognizable elements of *Caddyshack* into participatory stunts.

### Baby Ruth pool gag
The candy-bar/kiddie-pool game directly evokes the film's famous pool sequence.

### Gopher Hunt
The gopher becomes **Paul O as a moving human target**, apparently with paintballs.

### Golf
The movie's sport becomes **Nut Putt**, with J Dubs serving as the physical risk/target.

This is characteristic R&F event design:

> take familiar pop culture → replace fictional characters with people from the show's own universe → let the resulting discomfort become the real entertainment.
