## Why Bergen County matters

Regional bar nights show that the R&F community did not organize only around large official events such as the Big ASS Bash.

It had smaller geographic nodes:

- Manhattan/Times Square;
- Northern New Jersey/Bergen County;
- Washington/Arlington;
- Fairfax-era locations;
- Long Island and other early RFNet social areas.

These gatherings are useful because their photographs often reveal the **ordinary repeat population** rather than only hosts and headline characters.

## Hottub as documentation node

Hottub's role as uploader matters. His surviving account carried more than 41,000 posts and appears around multiple community events.

This strengthens the idea that some RFNet members functioned as informal local correspondents/photographers for the social network.
