## The tradition appears to predate 2007

The strongest oral-history evidence comes from **spoon's 2016 memorial for GVac**.

Spoon remembers roughly sixteen years of friendship beginning with:

- early rf.net jokes around 2000/01;
- **2001 bar crawls**;
- sports-team arguments;
- **“that massive softball comeback”**;
- many R&F events.

Source:
- https://www.doctorsteve.com/gvac-memorial/

Evidence grade: **C — direct participant recollection.**

The wording strongly suggests a memorable softball game involving GVac occurred well before the 2007 gallery set, though the exact year, teams and comeback remain unresolved.

## Later former-member description: “family type events”

A 2022 former-fan discussion asks what happened to the old WNEW-era figures. One participant reflects that old R&F people remained peripherally in touch and explicitly remembers the community organizing:

- Christmas parties;
- **the softball game**;
- other “family type events” outside officially show-sponsored appearances.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/tbrkyq/does_anyone_know_where_the_ron_and_fez_characters/

Evidence grade: **C/E participant-community recollection.**

This is important because it characterizes softball not as a station promotion but as something the **community itself organized**.

## Why softball matters

Softball is almost the perfect social-history event because it requires more organization than “meet us at a bar.”

Someone must arrange:

- field access;
- teams;
- equipment;
- dates/times;
- transportation;
- player rosters;
- spectators;
- food/drinks;
- photographs.

That makes softball evidence of a community with enough cohesion to **self-organize structured offline recreation**.

The repeated memories of softball also span multiple phases of the fandom, suggesting it may have functioned like a community reunion tradition.

## GVac / spoon connection

The GVac memorial gives softball unusual emotional weight because it appears not as show lore but as a **friendship memory**.

Spoon's recollection places the softball comeback alongside:

- 2001 bar crawls;
- years of arguments/jokes;
- many R&F events;
- long-distance friendship after people moved away.

That means the game had survived in memory for roughly fifteen years not primarily because it was great radio, but because it was part of the participants' real lives.

## Core interpretation

RF Softball matters because it demonstrates the audience doing something **ordinary together**.

The bizarre fights, hypnosis and weddings show the community becoming entertainment. Softball shows it becoming something simpler and arguably more significant: **friends organizing weekends around one another because the radio show had introduced them.**
