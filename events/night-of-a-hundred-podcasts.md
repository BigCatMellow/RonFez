# Night of a Hundred / Thousand Podcasts

## Classification

**XM-era fan-media showcase / friends-of-show distribution experiment / proto-network event / community-to-broadcast pipeline**

## Name uncertainty

East Side Dave, revisiting the event in 2026, remembers that R&F did the concept more than once and says the name may have been **Night of a Hundred Podcasts** or **Night of a Thousand Podcasts**. In the specific 2007 episode he revisits, Dave believes they were calling it **Night of a Hundred Podcasts**.

Source:
- Davey Mac's *Tales From The Satellite*, Episode LXXI, “Fun & Ron & Fez,” June 21, 2026
- transcript: https://podscan.fm/podcasts/davey-macs-tales-from-the-satellite/episodes/episode-lxxi-fun-amp-ron-amp-fez

Because the naming is uncertain even in Dave's participant recollection, preserve both names until the original 2007 broadcast is indexed.

## What the concept was

Dave describes Ron's idea as using SiriusXM time that might otherwise have been filled with *Best Of Ron & Fez* to give **friends of the show who had podcasts their own SiriusXM airtime** over a Friday night/weekend block.

This is historically striking.

The flow is:

`R&F COMMUNITY MEMBER → MAKES OWN PODCAST → PITCHES R&F → GETS NATIONAL SATELLITE AIRTIME`

The audience is no longer merely generating material *for* Ron & Fez.

R&F is now functioning as a **distribution platform for audience-generated shows**.

## The 2007 episode context

Dave's 2026 retrospective says the original R&F episode he was revisiting contained an extraordinary amount of simultaneous activity:

- people pitching their podcasts for the showcase;
- Mikey D and his daughter becoming part of a controversy/pitch;
- Mikeyboy;
- Mooch;
- Jay Mohr-related chaos;
- Dave and Earl preparing to play one-on-one basketball after the show for a **$1,500** bet according to the original audio Dave re-heard;
- a Brooklyn crew apparently filming material connected to a proposed **Ron & Fez television show**.

This makes the day less an isolated podcast stunt than a snapshot of the show's mature “everything is happening at once” ecosystem.

## Friends-of-show media economy

By this point, numerous people around R&F were producing their own media:

- podcasts;
- internet radio;
- fan songs;
- Weird Medicine;
- High Society Radio;
- community videos;
- photographs;
- board-created shows and offshoots.

Night of a Hundred Podcasts formalized something that had been growing informally for years:

> **R&F's audience contained aspiring and actual broadcasters.**

## Relationship to earlier RFNet infrastructure

There is a clear progression:

### 2001–02
Fans build message boards, soundboards, image packs and event archives.

### WJFK
Fans help distribute the show through internet streams and MP3s.

### XM
Fans launch podcasts and secondary shows.

### Night of a Hundred Podcasts
Ron uses satellite-radio inventory to put some of those fan/community productions onto SiriusXM itself.

The feedback loop becomes a **media incubator**.

## Why this matters historically

This predates the mature contemporary podcast ecosystem in which every comedian, producer and fan-adjacent personality can easily launch a show and cross-promote it.

R&F was already experimenting with:

- community-generated audio;
- cross-promotion;
- alternative distribution;
- weekend network programming;
- friends-of-show media brands.

It is therefore important not merely as a goofy promotion but as part of the show's transition from “radio program with fans” to **small media ecosystem**.

## Core interpretation

Night of a Hundred Podcasts may be one of the clearest examples of the R&F secondary universe becoming a **media network rather than simply a fandom**.

The listener's path could now be:

`LISTEN → JOIN COMMUNITY → DEVELOP A PERSONA → CREATE A SHOW → PITCH R&F → AIR ON SIRIUSXM`

That is an extraordinary amount of permeability between audience and broadcaster.
