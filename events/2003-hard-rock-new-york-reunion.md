# Hard Rock Cafe — New York Reunion, 2003

## Classification

**WJFK-era return-to-New-York live event / WNEW alumni reunion / Crankcase performance / O&A crossover controversy / evidence that the New York fan network survived the DC relocation**

## Date and venue status

Surviving R&F historical summaries identify the event as **Hard Rock Cafe — New York Reunion (2003)**.

The exact calendar date and exact Hard Rock branch/address have not yet been independently recovered in this pass. Given the New York setting and later R&F history, Times Square is an obvious hypothesis, but **do not assign Times Square without a contemporary source**.

## Current reconstructed event

The long-maintained show history says:

- Ron and Fez returned to New York for a **Crankcase gig** at the Hard Rock Cafe;
- they played to a **packed upstairs room**;
- WNEW-era alumni appeared, including:
  - **Hawk**
  - **Tasteless Ginny**
  - **Mikey D**
  - **Tenacious C**
  - **Billy Staples**
- many New York friends of the show attended;
- **Anthony Cumia made a surprise appearance**;
- the room erupted into a large **O&A chant**;
- WJFK then reportedly pulled the broadcast for more than an hour and aired a **Best Of** under the explanation “technical difficulties.”

Source:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_and_Fez

**Evidence grade: B/D-tertiary pending original tape, station logs or contemporary threads.**

## Why the event matters

R&F's 2003 move to Fairfax created a basic historical question:

> Did the old New York society dissolve when the live show moved away?

This event strongly suggests **no**.

The remembered room is explicitly populated by:

- old staff;
- old civilian personalities;
- New York fans;
- Anthony Cumia;
- the new WJFK broadcast operation.

The event therefore acts as a bridge between the WNEW civilization and the emerging Fairfax one.

## “Packed upstairs room”

The room's remembered crowd size is important because it indicates that even after months of Washington-only broadcasting, R&F could still mobilize the New York fanbase.

This fits the parallel evidence that fan-run streaming/MP3 distribution helped out-of-market listeners continue following the WJFK show.

The New York community had not been fully severed from the daily program.

## Relationship to fan distribution

By 2003–05, out-of-market fans later remember using:

- Big ASS Antenna;
- Live365;
- Radio BBQ;
- circulated MP3s.

If the New York crowd at this event was still current on R&F continuity despite the DC signal, the reunion may be a concrete demonstration of that unofficial distribution network's social consequence.

This is a hypothesis to test through attendee recollections, not yet a proven causal statement.

## Core interpretation

The New York Reunion is historically important because it shows that **geography had stopped defining the R&F audience**. The show had physically relocated to Fairfax, but the old New York cast, fans and rival-radio relationships could still reassemble instantly when Ron and Fez came home.
