## Date caution

The RFNet restoration/gallery system sometimes carries upload/restoration dates rather than the original event date.

Therefore the safest present wording is:

> **Bergen County Bar Night existed by May 21, 2007 and was photographed/uploaded into the RFNet event gallery.**

Do not assume May 21 was the actual night until a thread, show reference, photo EXIF, or independent event notice confirms it.

## Why this matters

This is one of the smaller events that would disappear from a conventional radio history because it may not have been a major broadcast remote.

Its historical value is precisely that it shows the mature R&F community had enough density to support ordinary regional gatherings that warranted their own named gallery category.

That means the social calendar was not limited to:

- Big ASS Bash;
- weddings;
- official station events;
- Ron/Fez-hosted spectacles.

There were also lower-stakes bar nights organized around the community itself.
