# B.B. King's XM kickoff / New York welcome-back party — September 9, 2005

## Classification

**Transition event / O&A–R&F reunion / fan gathering / XM launch ritual / New York return marker**

## What is established

The strongest surviving synthesis places the event on **September 9, 2005**, three days before *Ron & Fez* began their regular XM program on September 12.

The gathering took place at **B.B. King's Blues Cafe in Times Square, New York City**.

The account says the four hosts of the two shows — Ron Bennington, Fez Whatley, Opie Hughes and Anthony Cumia — were there, along with O&A co-host **Jim Norton**, and that **hundreds of fans** attended.

It is described simultaneously as:

- a **preshow kickoff party**;
- and a **welcome-back-to-New-York party for Ron and Fez**.

Sources:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_and_Fez
- https://www.wikizero.org/wiki/en/Ron_and_Fez

Evidence grade: **B/C synthesis** pending contemporary venue/XM announcement or audio.

---

## Why this date matters

The event sits in the small gap between two eras:

- July 29, 2005 — final WJFK broadcast;
- August 2 — XM move publicly announced;
- September 8–9 — R&F appear on O&A discussing old WNEW history and the years apart;
- **September 9 — B.B. King's fan gathering**;
- September 12 — regular R&F XM show begins.

That makes the party almost a **ritual re-entry into the New York radio/fan ecosystem**.

The physical audience was being told, in effect:

> the old WNEW neighborhood is partly back together — but this time on satellite.

---

## O&A/R&F reconnection

The party should be treated as more than a promotional appearance.

The week itself reconnected:

- Ron;
- Fez;
- Opie;
- Anthony;
- Jim Norton;
- the WNEW-era fan population;
- Washington-era fans who had followed the show;
- XM subscribers/new listeners.

That makes September 2005 a bridge where previously separate fan histories began to overlap again.
