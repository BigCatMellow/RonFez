# “Rogue” Christmas Party at the Crow's Nest — probably 2009

## Classification

**Fan-organized Christmas/bar-night gathering / RFNet social event / Fez-as-Santa oral history / non-official community party**

## Current source status

The event is currently preserved through a detailed **2022 firsthand attendee recollection**, not a recovered contemporary RFNet thread.

A participant writes that their first R&F bar night was the infamous:

> **“Rogue” Christmas party at the Crow's Nest on the Upper East Side in NYC**

They remember:

- meeting **GVac** outside;
- **Fez dressed as Santa**, wearing a bad toupee;
- meeting many `.net` regulars;
- some attendees traveling from as far west as **Montana**;
- the party continuing most of the night;
- **Ron eventually arriving with his wife and HTG**;
- friendships from the gathering surviving many years later.

When asked the year, the attendee replies:

> **“I believe this was 2009.”**

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/snvpaf

Evidence grade: **C/E — apparent firsthand participant memory, but date explicitly uncertain.**

## Why “Rogue” matters

The remembered name strongly suggests the party was somehow **unofficial, alternate or fan-organized** rather than the principal R&F/RFNet Christmas event.

That interpretation is plausible but not yet established.

Possible meanings:

- party organized independently by RFNet users;
- competing Christmas gathering;
- deliberately unofficial after-show/bar night;
- nickname arising from a board dispute;
- event that happened without formal R&F sponsorship.

Do not define “Rogue” until the original planning thread is located.

## The Crow's Nest

The attendee identifies the venue as **the Crow's Nest on the Upper East Side**.

Exact address/business identity remains unresolved. New York has had multiple establishments with similar names, and retrospective memory alone is insufficient to pin the venue.

Research should search 2008–10 NYC business/event listings and original RFNet references before assigning an address.

The most vivid remembered image is Fez outside the venue dressed as Santa and wearing a terrible toupee.

Whether Fez was:

The attendee remembers people coming from as far as **Montana**.

That is a striking claim and should be checked against attendee handles/threads.

## Ron, wife, and HTG

The attendee remembers Ron arriving later with:

- his wife;
- **Happy Typing Girl / Debbie Wolf**.

This should be treated as a firsthand social-memory claim, not used to infer private relationship details.

## Long-term friendship testimony

The attendee's most important statement may not be any celebrity detail. They say several people met through that scene remained friends years later and describe GVac and Fez as having had a tremendous impact on their life.

That reinforces the larger RFNet finding:

> for some participants, the lasting product of R&F was not the archive or favorite bit — it was the **friendship network**.

## Core interpretation

The Rogue Christmas party is currently oral history, not a fully reconstructed event. But the recollection captures something central to the mature R&F lifestyle: **fans were organizing nights substantial enough to draw people across states, with hosts and longtime civilian personalities drifting in and out of what was fundamentally their own social world.**
