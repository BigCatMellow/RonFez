# Christmas Party 2002 — unresolved event dossier

## Classification

**The first RFNet Christmas/X-Mas party is now independently remembered as a 2002 event and is directly represented by a restored RFNet gallery category; relationship to `CyberSoldier Holiday Party Pics 2002` remains unresolved.**

## Direct gallery evidence

The restored RonFez.net Photo Gallery contains a surviving category breadcrumb:

> `Home » Main » Events » Christmas Party 2002`

One indexed image in that category is titled:

> **Chris the Cop**

Direct page:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/527/ppuser/21562

The page currently shows:

- gallery uploader/curator: **mikeyboy**;
- displayed upload date: **May 18, 2007**;
- keyword: `Chris Cop`;
- a later 2007 comment by Don Stugots.

The 2007 gallery date should be treated as restoration/migration metadata, not as the original party date. The category title itself explicitly supplies **2002**.

## What the gallery establishes

At minimum:

1. RFNet's restored gallery preserved a discrete event category called **Christmas Party 2002**;
2. the category contains at least one photograph identified as **Chris the Cop**;
3. Mikeyboy was involved in the later gallery migration/curation;
4. a 2002 physical-community Christmas event was important enough to be retained as its own gallery category.

## Independent 2023 community corroboration: `First RF.NET X-Mas party`

A January 16, 2023 post in the Ron & Fez subreddit is explicitly titled:

> **First RF.NET X-Mas party**

The post was a **shared image/link**, apparently surfaced during the restored-archive period.

One apparent participant says, in substance, that they were grateful the photograph was labeled because they barely recognized themselves, explicitly dates it to **2002**, and thanks JustJon for restoring the archive.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/ronandfez/comments/10dvfb9

### Why this matters

This is independent of the restored gallery breadcrumb and materially improves the event reconstruction.

The two source surfaces now line up:

`restored RFNet gallery category: Christmas Party 2002`

+

`2023 old-community image discussion: First RF.NET X-Mas party / participant identifies it as 2002`

That supports the statement:

> **RFNet's first Christmas/X-Mas party was in 2002.**

### Evidence caution

The 2023 recollection is roughly twenty years after the event and should not be treated as primary documentation by itself. But the writer speaks as someone visible in the labeled image, and the date independently matches RFNet's surviving archival category title.

Current grade:

- **2002 first-party identification: B/C-D hybrid** — direct archive category plus apparent-participant retrospective corroboration.

The Reddit result does **not** currently expose the shared target URL through the available research environment, so it has not yet been proven which exact restored RFNet image/file was posted.

## Venue lead: Dave & Buster's at the Palisades mall

The same 2023 discussion contains a much more specific physical-world memory.

A commenter asks whether the first party was the one at:

> **Dave & Buster's at the Palisades mall**

Another reply discusses people being out in the **game room**, consistent with a Dave & Buster's-type venue, and treats the venue memory as familiar rather than introducing a new theory.

The geographic reading of this recollection is most plausibly:

> **Dave & Buster's at Palisades Center, West Nyack, New York**

### Evidence boundary

This is still retrospective participant/community memory, not a contemporary event listing, receipt, invitation, RFNet announcement, or broadcast.

Therefore the safe status is:

- **Dave & Buster's / Palisades Center venue: C/E lead**;
- **West Nyack identification: geographic interpretation of that venue lead**;
- **not yet promoted to established event fact**.

The event file should continue to say `venue unresolved` until a contemporary RFNet thread, invitation, audio reference, or external event record confirms it.

## Relationship to `CyberSoldier Holiday Party Pics 2002`

Separately, the restored File Library's global `Most Popular Files` block contains:

> **CyberSoldier Holiday Party Pics 2002**

with roughly 10,000+ accumulated downloads.

That creates an increasingly plausible hypothesis:

> `Christmas Party 2002`, the remembered **first RF.NET X-Mas party**, and `CyberSoldier Holiday Party Pics 2002` may all document the same event/photo cluster.

The first-party date is no longer the weak part of the hypothesis: **2002 is now independently supported**. The missing bridge is CyberSoldier's role and the actual file/gallery relationship.

Possible models include:

1. CyberSoldier hosted the first RFNet Christmas party;
2. CyberSoldier photographed or packaged pictures from the broader first RFNet Christmas party;
3. CyberSoldier contributed one photo set while other photographers contributed material to the same event;
4. the File Library item and gallery category were separate 2002 holiday gatherings;
5. `CyberSoldier Holiday Party Pics 2002` was later imported into the generic `Christmas Party 2002` gallery category without preserving its original File Library title.

Do not collapse these possibilities until a direct source connects CyberSoldier to the event.

## Core interpretation

The 2002 Christmas event is no longer merely a mysterious gallery category.

We now have a direct archive category, later participant-style identification of the **first RFNet X-Mas party as 2002**, a venue lead, a partially reconstructed civilian roster, and testimony that **three group photographs** from the gathering were preserved.

That significantly narrows two previously opaque RFNet mysteries at once:

- `CyberSoldier Holiday Party Pics 2002` may belong to this event cluster;
- `The Group Photo` may be one of its group images.

Neither identification is proven yet. But the investigation has moved from isolated file titles to a specific event, probable venue, people network, and recoverable set of photographs.
