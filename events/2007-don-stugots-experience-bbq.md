# The Don Stugots “Experiene” BBQ — 2007

## Classification

**Fan/community-organized social event / RFNet gallery category / Hottub photo set / evidence that the civilian universe generated its own events**

## Naming artifact

The surviving RonFez.net breadcrumb/category is spelled:

> **The Don Stugots Experiene BBQ**

The apparent misspelling `Experiene` is preserved deliberately because it appears in the archive text. Do not silently “correct” historical metadata when the oddity itself may be part of the original page/category.

## Date status

Hottub-uploaded images in the category are dated:

> **Monday, June 11, 2007**

Examples:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2565/ppuser/44981
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2562/ppuser/18105

The gallery date proves the photos were in the system by that date. It does **not yet prove the BBQ itself occurred June 11**. The event may have taken place during the preceding weekend or earlier.

Therefore:

- category/event existence: **A/D**
- June 11 gallery date: **A**
- exact BBQ date: **U pending announcement/audio**

## Why this event matters

The title does not say:

> Ron & Fez BBQ.

It says:

> **Don Stugots Experience BBQ.**

That distinction is crucial.

By 2007, the secondary universe had enough internal identity that a fan/show-adjacent personality could apparently have a named event with its own RFNet photo category.

The fan community had developed:

- its own hosts;
- its own event brands;
- its own photographers;
- its own documentation;
- its own audience drawn from the same social network.

This is evidence that the community was no longer dependent on Ron and Fez physically appearing in order to produce a culturally meaningful R&F-world event.

## Relationship to “The Don Stugots Experience”

The category name strongly implies an existing **Don Stugots Experience** identity/project.

Full gallery enumeration should recover:

## Timing coincidence with Rock-a-Hula aftermath

The photo upload date, June 11, 2007, is also the date R&F broadcast its **Rock-a-Hula Luau review**.

That does not establish that the Don Stugots BBQ and Rock-a-Hula were connected. The close archival timing should simply be preserved for later event-calendar reconstruction.

The Don Stugots BBQ is obscure precisely in the way that makes it valuable. It shows the R&F “secondary universe” **running its own social programming**.
