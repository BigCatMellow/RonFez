## The event began as a smaller private plan

A near-contemporary 2010 essay by longtime listener/friend **Scott Hudson** says Dave and Casey originally planned a **small, quiet wedding** after Dave proposed to Casey on-air.

Hudson says **Ron convinced Dave** to abandon that plan in favor of a large party/broadcast from the Hard Rock Cafe in Times Square.

Source:
- https://scotthudson.blogspot.com/2010/06/save-east-side-dave.html

**Evidence grade: B/C — near-contemporary account by an identified friend/listener.**

This dramatically changes the event's interpretation.

The show did not merely broadcast an already-public wedding.

According to this account, **the radio show helped transform the scale and nature of the wedding itself**.

---

## Private life → show infrastructure

The sequence becomes:

1. Dave and Casey have a real relationship.
2. Dave proposes on the radio.
3. Couple initially contemplates a small/private wedding.
4. Ron pushes for a larger public event.
5. Hard Rock Times Square becomes the venue.
6. R&F staff/fans/community become participants/audience.
7. RFNet photographers document the ceremony.
8. The wedding becomes future serialized continuity.
9. Fez continues carrying a grievance about the aftermath **two years later**.

This may be one of the strongest examples of the program changing the actual shape of a participant's life rather than merely commenting on it.

## Fez's role — helped organize and run the wedding

A **June 22, 2009** near-contemporary fan archive by Dan says that around Dave and Casey's second wedding anniversary, Fez was still upset that he had not been invited to the **family dinner that followed the wedding**, despite having **helped organize and run the wedding**.

The archive says Fez mentioned the grievance on the Friday show, and it carried into Monday June 22, where other topics eventually reignited Dave/Fez animosity.

Source:
- https://philaflavaforum.com/forum/viewtopic.php?start=780&t=96299

**Evidence grade: D — near-contemporary fan segment log.**

This is a major relationship detail.

Fez was apparently not just a guest/co-host at the event; he had a meaningful organizational role, and the social boundary between the public show wedding and private family dinner later became emotionally significant to him.

## The two-year afterlife

The 2009 Dan archive shows how R&F continuity worked:

A **2007 wedding seating/invitation issue** remained available material in **2009**.

This means the real narrative unit wasn't:

> “wedding episode.”

It was:

> **Dave / Casey / Fez relationship history extending across years.**

A newcomer hearing Fez complain in 2009 would need to know what happened after the 2007 Hard Rock event. Longtime listeners already carried that context.

## Core interpretation

The Dave/Casey wedding is one of the clearest examples of why R&F cannot be understood as merely “radio about people's lives.” **The program altered the event itself.** A private milestone became a Hard Rock broadcast and community gathering; fan photographers turned it into a public archive; and the social boundaries created by that transformation were still producing genuine-seeming conflict two years later.
