## Why this is a near-perfect off-air→on-air case

The luau gives us exactly the process this project is trying to document:

1. A physical social event happens outside the normal studio.
2. Staff and friends of the show interact without the conversation being fully controlled by the broadcast.
3. Something involving Lilly goes wrong.
4. Earl apparently mishandles or withholds information.
5. Dave and Pitzy have a separate interpersonal problem involving Jonathan.
6. On the next show, Ron converts the event's social aftermath into radio.

This is not merely “R&F held a party.”

It is a **story-generation machine**.
