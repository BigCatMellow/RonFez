# Artifact — Big ASS Night of Fights ticket

## Event

**Big ASS Night of Fights I — Friday, February 22, 2002**

This surviving physical ticket materially strengthens the event record because it supplies venue, address, admission language, age restriction, doors time, station branding, and the exact public-facing event title.

## Visible ticket text

The surviving ticket reads, in substance:

- `RON & FEZ PRESENTS`
- `BIG ASS NIGHT OF FIGHTS`
- **Downtown Bar & Club**
- **190 Main Street, Farmingdale, NY**
- **Friday Feb. 22nd**
- **Doors open at 5 PM**
- **21 and over**
- **This ticket does not guarantee admission**
- directions via **WNEW.com**
- prominent **102.7 WNEW** branding on both sides

The design uses a yellow field, red boxing-glove illustrations, and green/black lettering.

## Why the fine print matters

`THIS TICKET DOES NOT GUARANTEE ADMISSION` is not disposable boilerplate in the context of R&F live-event history. Later Night of Fights material includes capacity/overflow problems, and fan memories repeatedly emphasize unexpectedly large crowds. The first event's ticket already anticipates that a ticket-holder might not get inside.

This does **not** prove there was an overflow problem at Night I; it only proves the promoter explicitly reserved against guaranteed entry.

## Venue correction / upgrade

Earlier event notes had the venue unresolved. The ticket now gives:

> **Downtown Bar & Club, 190 Main Street, Farmingdale, New York**

That should be treated as A/B-level artifact evidence for the advertised venue.

Future work should determine:

- the venue's legal/business name in 2002;
- capacity;
- floor plan;
- whether the fight area was a ring, improvised ring, or other setup;
- whether Downtown Bar & Club appears in Nassau County entertainment listings;
- photographs of the exterior/interior;
- whether Night of Fights II used the same location;
- whether the 5 PM door time preceded a later broadcast start.

## Tiny-detail ledger

- The ticket says **“ADMIT ONE”** in both top corners.
- WNEW logos appear at both bottom corners.
- The public title uses `BIG ASS NIGHT OF FIGHTS`, not “Friday Night Fights.”
- Directions were delegated to the station website rather than printed as a map.
- Admission was explicitly **21+**.
- Doors opened unusually early for a Friday-night bar event: **5 PM**.
- Ticket possession did not guarantee entry.

These details are preserved because physical ephemera often answers questions that no surviving audio bothers to state.
