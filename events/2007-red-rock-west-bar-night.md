# Red Rock West Bar Night — 2007

## Classification

**XM-era physical fan/social gathering / RonFez.net photo-documented event / civilian-cast social map**

## Date status

The surviving RonFez.net gallery category is titled **Red Rock West Bar Night**. Indexed photo pages show a site date around **May 26, 2007**, but this may be the image upload/date rather than the night itself.

Therefore:

> **Event year: 2007 — strong. Exact event date: not yet verified.**

Do not convert the gallery's image-post date into the event date until the event announcement or adjacent broadcast establishes it.

## Why this group photo matters

The grouping mixes:

- the hosts;
- high-volume RFNet people;
- fan/community personalities;
- figures whose radio importance is otherwise difficult to reconstruct.

In an ordinary radio history, names like Kevin Ogre or Smiler Grogan might disappear entirely because they were not salaried staff. A gallery caption proves that they occupied the same physical community space as the show and other known fans.

## Snoogans

Searchable RFNet profile/gallery metadata associated with **Snoogans** shows:

- registration: **January 2003**;
- location: **Mahwah, New Jersey**;
- approximately **36,871 posts** in a surviving snapshot.

This makes Snoogans a high-priority community biography. A 36k-post account photographed at a show social event is not an incidental listener in the sociological sense, even if the person had limited microphone time.

## Smiler Grogan

Smiler Grogan is also visible in later ordinary RFNet discussion. One surviving 2010 technology thread captures the account talking about choosing a phone while the user's wife was in labor.

That tiny detail is historically useful because it shows how board handles represented **whole lives**, not merely radio opinions. Years after appearing in an R&F event gallery, the same identities were still sharing ordinary life events in the site's general forums.

## Faith

Faith appears in both the Ron/Fez group and the larger fan grouping. This suggests social centrality at this particular event, but her exact relationship to the show/community needs sourcing before assigning a biography.

Research questions:

- first on-air mention;

## Bobo

Bobo's appearance here provides physical-event evidence supplementing existing music evidence: the RFNet March 2005 highlights archive explicitly lists a **Bobo Dan Rather song**.

The combination suggests a fan/community creator who was both producing material and attending social events.

## Mikeyboy as archive node

Mikeyboy appears both in the photos and, across the broader gallery, as a major uploader/custodian. This dual role matters:

> He was documenting a social scene he was also participating in.

That is different from a detached photographer or a station publicity archive.

## Venue

The exact Red Rock West location/address has not yet been established in this pass. New York had venues/businesses using similar names, so no address should be inferred from memory alone.

Research targets:

- original event announcement;
- venue website/Wayback listing;
- gallery descriptions with street/location;
- show audio around May 2007;
- staff mentions of how the bar night was organized.

## Off-air → on-air questions

The next important step is determining what happened *after* the photos:

- Did Ron discuss Faith, Snoogans, Bobo, Kevin Ogre, or Smiler Grogan afterward?
- Did any bar interaction become Board Gossip?
- Was there an argument, hookup, drunken incident, or running joke?
- Did Dave report anything from the night?

## Core interpretation

Red Rock West is valuable precisely because it is not one of the giant famous events. It exposes the **ordinary physical mesh** underneath the show: the people who went out together, recognized one another by screen name, and accumulated enough shared history that later on-air shorthand could assume listeners already knew them.
