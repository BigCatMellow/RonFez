## Why this matters

By June 2007 Ron & Fez had been gone from WJFK for almost two years, yet the old Washington-area community still had enough coherence to justify a specifically branded **DC Bar Night**.

That means the move to satellite did not simply erase the Fairfax audience.

The social geography became:

- New York studio/base;
- national satellite audience;
- surviving DC/Northern Virginia local community;
- RFNet as the organizing/archive layer.
