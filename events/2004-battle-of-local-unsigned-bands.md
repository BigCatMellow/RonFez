# Battle of the Local, Unsigned Bands — 2004

## Classification

**WJFK-era live-music showcase / local-scene bridge / fan discovery event / under-researched music-history node**

## Current evidence

Surviving R&F historical summaries state:

> **Battle of the Local, Unsigned Bands (2004)** — Ron and Fez hosted a showcase of unsigned area bands.

Sources:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_and_Fez
- https://www.wikizero.org/wiki/en/Ron_and_Fez

Evidence grade: **B/D secondary synthesis only**.

No complete band list, venue, exact date, winner or surviving official event page has yet been recovered.

---

## Why this matters more than the one-line summary suggests

The event belongs directly in the show's music history.

R&F did not only use music as:

- openers;
- closers;
- rejoiners;
- character themes;
- comedy-song contributions.

At least once in Fairfax they apparently used their audience/event apparatus to **showcase local unsigned bands**.

That makes the show a small piece of the Washington-area/local-band discovery ecosystem.

---

## Monty Love comparison

A separate 2003 event trail identifies **Monty Love**, a real Staten Island punk/pop-punk/hardcore band, performing in the R&F event world.

That doesn't establish Monty Love played the 2004 Battle of the Local, Unsigned Bands, but it proves the show already had precedent for placing real regional independent bands inside comedy-radio events.
