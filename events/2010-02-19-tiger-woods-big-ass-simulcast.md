## What “simulcast” meant in R&F terms

R&F's Big ASS Simulcast format was not a conventional rights-holding simulcast. The basic comic device was to have listeners watch a major live television event while Ron, Fez and staff supplied their own running audio commentary.

That transformed television into a shared radio/community event:

`national live event + R&F commentary + listeners watching along = alternate broadcast`

The Tiger Woods apology was unusually suited to this because the press event itself was already a mass-media spectacle.

---

## Historical context

On February 19, 2010, Woods made a nationally televised public statement amid the scandal surrounding his extramarital affairs.

R&F's archived clip establishes that the show reacted in real time rather than merely discussing the press conference later.

This is one of the clearest examples of the program acting like a **live commentary track for contemporary culture**.
