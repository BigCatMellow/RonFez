# Fez charity 5Ks — 2004 and 2005

## Classification

**WJFK-era charity/community participation / Fez physical-event tradition / under-documented fan gathering**

## What is currently supported

The surviving R&F historical synthesis states that:

> **Fez raced in an annual charity 5K with fans of the show in both 2004 and 2005.**

Source:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_and_Fez

The same source explicitly flags the larger WJFK appearances section as lacking adequate citations, so this claim should be treated as a **research lead rather than settled primary-source history**.

Evidence grade: **D/B secondary synthesis; primary source required.**

---

## Why this matters

Even in its current thinly sourced form, the 5K tradition fits a well-documented broader pattern from the Fairfax years:

- listeners attending pool/poker/fight events;
- Fez meeting fans socially at Friday Night Lights;
- Crazy Jen and other civilians participating physically;
- Don the Hypnotist crossing between studio and real-life contact;
- WJFK-area fans building a repeat social network around the show.

A charity race is qualitatively different from a bar appearance because it implies:

- repeated annual organization;
- a charitable beneficiary;
- fan participation outside a drinking/comedy setting;
- Fez himself physically taking part.

If confirmed, it would broaden the image of the Fairfax community considerably.

---

## What we do **not** yet know

No reliable source has yet been recovered for:

- charity name;
- race name;
- exact dates;
- course/location;
- registration page;
- how many R&F fans participated;
- whether Ron ran;
- whether WJFK formally sponsored it;
- whether the 2004 and 2005 events were the same race;
- whether photographs or results survive.

Do not invent any of these from general DC-area race calendars.

---

## Research strategy

Search should prioritize:

1. 2004/2005 WJFK event calendars.
2. RFNet threads containing `5K`, `race`, `Fez run`, `charity run` and likely date ranges.
3. Fairfax/Northern Virginia race-result archives.
4. Washington Post local-event listings.
5. full-show audio in spring/fall 2004 and 2005 for race training/results.
6. participant photo galleries.
7. later Fez/Ron references to running with listeners.

A particularly useful primary artifact would be a race-results page listing **Todd Hillier / Fez Whatley** by name, but identity matching should only be done where the context is clear.

---

## Relationship to Friday Night Lights

The 2005 5K and Friday Night Lights both belong to the last WJFK year and both place Fez physically among listeners.

That makes them complementary institutions:

- **5K:** public/charitable/physical participation;
- **Friday Night Lights:** informal weekly social gathering.

Together they suggest that “Fez as community host” extended beyond bars and radio remotes.

## Core interpretation

The charity 5Ks are currently one of the thinner event leads in the archive, so the correct historical statement is narrow:

> **A long-standing R&F event history says Fez ran annual charity 5Ks with fans in 2004 and 2005; the exact races have not yet been independently identified.**

That uncertainty is preserved intentionally.
