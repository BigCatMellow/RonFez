# Mooch Farewell Party — May 2007

## Classification

**XM-era staff/community farewell / RonFez.net-documented physical gathering / fan-photography archive**

## Direct evidence

RonFez.net preserves a dedicated gallery category titled:

> **Mooch Farewell Party**

Multiple gallery pages are labeled:

> **Mooch Farewell Pics Taken by Fallon**

and were uploaded/curated under Mikeyboy's account.

Examples:
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2396/ppuser/37449
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2412/size/big/cat/ppuser/36766
- https://www.ronfez.net/gallery/showphoto.php/photo/2383/ppuser/24511

The surviving pages display **May 28, 2007**.

## Date caution

As with other restored gallery pages, May 28 is securely the visible gallery date but not yet independently proven as the actual party date.

Current safe wording:

> **Mooch's farewell party was documented on RFNet by May 28, 2007, with photographs taken by Fallon and curated/uploaded by Mikeyboy.**

## Mikeyboy's archival role

The chain here is unusually clear:

`Fallon takes photographs → Mikeyboy uploads/curates them → RFNet preserves the farewell as a named event category`

This strengthens the interpretation of Mikeyboy as a **community media custodian**, not merely a prolific photographer.

## Why the farewell matters

Mooch was part of the XM production/social world. A farewell party given enough importance to receive its own gallery category indicates that staff departures could also become **community milestones**.

The boundary between workplace and fan world was porous:

- staff worked together;
- fans knew staff by name/persona;
- staff departures mattered socially;
- fans attended/photographed gatherings;
- the board archived the event afterward.

The Mooch farewell is a small event with large structural value. It shows the community preserving **staff lifecycle events** the same way a workplace friend group, club, or extended family would preserve them.
