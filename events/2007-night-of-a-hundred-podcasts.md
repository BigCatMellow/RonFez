# Night of a Hundred Podcasts — 2007

## Classification

**XM-era secondary-media event / friends-of-show programming experiment / podcast-to-satellite pipeline / dense studio-social day**

## Current best source

East Side Dave McDonald's 2026 oral-history podcast *Tales From The Satellite*, Episode LXXI (“Fun & Ron & Fez”), reconstructs a 2007 R&F day involving what he and his co-host identify as the:

> **Night of a Hundred Podcasts**

Dave initially wonders whether the recurring idea was called “Night of a Hundred” or “Night of a Thousand” podcasts; his co-host says **this particular one was the Night of a Hundred Podcasts**.

Source:
- https://podscan.fm/podcasts/davey-macs-tales-from-the-satellite/episodes/episode-lxxi-fun-amp-ron-amp-fez

Evidence grade: **C — direct participant retrospective, published June 2026.**

Exact original 2007 broadcast date remains unresolved.

---

## The concept

Dave explains the premise in unusually clear production terms.

Sirius/XM had weekend/Friday-night hours that might otherwise be filled with **Best Of Ron & Fez** programming. Ron's idea was to take friends of the show who had podcasts or independent programs and give some of them **actual SiriusXM airtime** instead.

That is historically significant because it formalizes a process already visible elsewhere in the R&F ecosystem:

`fan/friend → creates independent media → R&F notices it → independent media receives satellite platform`

The secondary universe briefly became **scheduled programming**.

---

## Why this is more than a podcast promotion

By 2007, the R&F civilian/media ecosystem included:

- message-board personalities;
- Paltalk users;
- callers;
- local/live-event regulars;
- people running podcasts or secondary radio shows;
- former interns/staff;
- comedians and show friends.

Night of a Hundred Podcasts turned that decentralized population into a kind of **incubator or variety network**.

The idea resembles, years before podcasts became fully mainstream, a host using spare channel capacity to give audience-adjacent creators access to a professional national platform.

---

## The day was apparently much denser than the title suggests

Dave's 2026 reconstruction says the same day included multiple overlapping R&F storylines and visitors.

Names he mentions include:

- **Mikey D**;
- Mikey D's daughter, who wanted to be involved in the podcast programming;
- **Mikeyboy**;
- **Mooch**;
- **Jay Mohr**;
- a group Dave remembers as Brooklyn guys including **Franklin, Dean and Joey**;
- many other people in what Dave describes as an extremely crowded studio.

Dave's central point is that a listener hearing the original program might remember it as an ordinary R&F day even though, from the staff perspective, an enormous amount was happening at once.

This is useful evidence for reconstructing **how dense the studio/social world could be without every visitor becoming a headline event**.

## Dave vs. Earl basketball — same-day afterlife

Dave says that after the show, he and Earl played a one-on-one basketball game for **$1,500**.

He remembers winning **12–2** and says Earl brought the cash.

Dave separately produced a 2024 *Tales From The Satellite* episode specifically titled around the **Dave vs. Earl R&F Hoops Championship**, providing an independent retrospective trail for the game.

Until the original 2007 tape/date is mapped, treat:

- game existence: **strong C-level participant evidence**;
- exact score/stakes: **C-level Dave recollection**;
- same-day relationship to Night of a Hundred Podcasts: **C-level 2026 recollection**.

Do not silently upgrade the score to A-level fact.

## “Hundred” versus “Thousand” naming problem

Dave's uncertainty is useful rather than inconvenient.

It suggests R&F may have used the concept more than once, perhaps under variant exaggerated titles.

Research should therefore search for:

- `Night of a Hundred Podcasts`;
- `Night of 100 Podcasts`;
- `Night of a Thousand Podcasts`;
- `Night of 1000 Podcasts`;
- weekend podcast takeover;
- friends-of-show podcast night.

Do not merge differently named events until original schedules/audio establish whether they were sequels, alternate labels or memory drift.

## Core interpretation

Night of a Hundred Podcasts may be one of the clearest examples of R&F treating the secondary universe not merely as **content about fans**, but as a **talent and media ecosystem worth putting on the actual satellite channel**.

In modern terms, Ron was temporarily turning a talk-radio show's social graph into a creator network.
