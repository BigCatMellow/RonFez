# Ron & Fez Pool Tournament — 2003

## Classification

**WJFK/Fairfax local appearance / billiards tournament / karaoke-comedy event / civilian-cast recruitment site**

## Date and venue status

The event is consistently listed in surviving R&F historical summaries as **Ron and Fez Pool Tournament (2003)**.

The exact date and venue have not yet been recovered from a primary event page, tape or ticket. Do not infer either from later annual pool-tournament locations.

**Current date precision: 2003 only.**

## Current reconstructed program

The long-maintained show history says the event included:

- a billiards tournament;
- **Fez performing “Harper Valley PTA”** as karaoke;
- **Cigar Sid** performing **“Sugar Sugar”**;
- **J Dubs' Hot Pepper Challenge**;
- **“Fairyoke”**, described as listeners performing karaoke songs selected/framed for effeminate comic effect.

Source:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_and_Fez

These details are useful but currently depend largely on fan-maintained historical synthesis rather than primary event audio.

**Evidence grade: B/D-tertiary pending original broadcast/gallery evidence.**

## Fez and “Harper Valley PTA”

Fez's association with **“Harper Valley PTA”** was not a one-off event gag. Independent cultural references note that Fez repeatedly sang his own version on R&F and that it was a song he had sung for his family as a child.

That makes the pool-tournament karaoke appearance part of **Fez's personal performance repertoire**, not just random karaoke selection.

Source lead:
- https://culture.fandom.com/wiki/Harper_Valley_PTA

For stronger documentation, recover original R&F discussions in which Fez explains the childhood/family connection.

## Cigar Sid / Sidcada and “Sugar Sugar”

Historical show summaries identify **Cigar Sid**, also called **Sidcada**, as one of the WJFK-era callers/studio regulars. His “Sugar Sugar” performance at the pool tournament gives us a concrete event role.

## J Dubs' Hot Pepper Challenge

The historical record names **J Dubs** as participant in a Hot Pepper Challenge.

This person later appears again in Fairfax event history:

- Fire and Ice Challenge with Crazy Jen at the 2003 Poker Tournament;
- boxing against El Jefe at the 2004 St. Pat's Spat.

That creates a multi-event civilian arc:

`HOT PEPPER CHALLENGE → FIRE AND ICE → BOXING MATCH`

The chronology strongly suggests J Dubs was not a one-off caller but a reusable physical-comedy personality.

## Why the event matters

This event comes during the period when R&F were building a new Fairfax-area cast after losing their daily New York physical base.

Its structure is revealing:

- ordinary billiards provides the public-event shell;
- karaoke exposes personalities;
- food/pain challenge turns a listener into a physical character;
- recurring local people get names and histories.

The event functions partly as **community entertainment and partly as informal casting**.

## Relationship to the 2nd Annual Pool Tournament

A **2nd Annual Ron and Fez Pool Tournament** occurred in 2004 and included Don the Hypnotist, Fez, Crazy Jen, football trivia and a listener-jingle contest.

That strongly implies the 2003 event was recognized as the first annual edition, though the surviving summary does not explicitly call it “1st Annual.”

Do not retroactively rename the 2003 event unless a contemporary promo uses that language.
