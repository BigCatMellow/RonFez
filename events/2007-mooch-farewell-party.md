# Mooch Farewell Party — 2007

## Classification

**Staff farewell / RFNet-photographed social gathering / civilian-staff crossover event**

## Direct evidence

Multiple surviving RonFez.net gallery pages carry the category/title:

> **Mooch Farewell Pics Taken by Fallon**

They were uploaded under **mikeyboy** but explicitly credit **Fallon** as photographer.

Visible metadata includes:

- gallery/upload date: **May 28, 2007**;
- uploader/curator: mikeyboy;
- photographer credit: Fallon;
- category visible elsewhere in the gallery as **Mooch Farewell Party**.

Sources:

Evidence grade: **A/D for party/category/photo custody; exact event date still unresolved.**

## Archive-custody chain

This event gives a particularly clear example of how RFNet's media archive worked:

1. **Fallon** takes photographs.
2. **Mikeyboy** uploads/curates them into RFNet.
3. Other members comment on/rate them.
4. The category survives years later as the visual record of a staff transition.

That supports the broader interpretation of Mikeyboy as a **media custodian**, not merely a photographer of his own material.

## Date problem

The May 28, 2007 gallery date falls only days after the Free FM format ended on May 24, but this does **not prove** the party occurred May 28 or that it was directly caused by the terrestrial show's cancellation.

Possible relationships:

- farewell from a particular staff role;
- departure from R&F/XM;
- end of Free FM-related work;
- move/geographic change;
- simply a gallery upload after an earlier event.

Do not infer the reason from chronology alone.

---

## Mooch's wider importance

Later show histories credit **Mooch Cassidy and Chris Stanley** with creating the elaborate pop-culture/music show openings used through much of the late-2000s period.

A 2008 Doctor Steve post refers warmly to **“my new best pal, ‘Mooch Cassidy’ from Belfast, Ireland”** while describing time with the XM 202 staff.

Source:
- https://www.doctorsteve.com/2008/02/06/weird-medicine-part-deux-in-the-can-so-to-speak/

That means the farewell-party category is part of a staff biography that still needs reconstruction; it should not be mistaken for proof that Mooch vanished permanently after 2007.

## Core interpretation

The Mooch party is a good example of why event titles must be treated carefully. A surviving category called **“farewell”** is direct evidence that the community understood some transition as a farewell; it is not, by itself, proof of a permanent departure. The photos nonetheless show staff milestones being absorbed into the same civilian archive as fan bar nights and weddings.
