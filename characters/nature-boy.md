# The Nature Boy — Fez Whatley

## Classification

**Recurring Ric Flair-inspired performance persona / wrestling-promo improvisation / major Fez signature character / cross-era evidence of Fez's active comic range**

## Performer

**Fez Whatley / Todd Hillier**

## Core historical importance

Nature Boy is one of the clearest corrections to the simplified late-era image of Fez as inherently passive or unable to carry radio.

In Nature Boy mode, Fez could command the room, sustain a long improvisational voice, work rhythmically against music, generate insults and boasts in real time, and use professional-wrestling promo grammar as a comic framework.

The persona therefore belongs alongside Fezetorials, Jan Brady and André the Giant as evidence of **early/mid-career Fez as an assertive performer**.

## Performance grammar

The persona borrows from Ric Flair's wrestling-promo style rather than operating as a literal biographical impersonation.

Recurring structural features include:

- shouted `Woooo!` punctuation;
- long escalating boasts;
- mock luxury/status imagery;
- insults toward opponents/targets;
- rhythmic call-and-response with Ron;
- wrestling-style self-mythology;
- adaptation of current show material into promo language;
- musical beds/intros in some versions.

The best classification is therefore:

> **Ric Flair-inspired promo persona, not simply voice mimicry.**

---

## Why it matters to Fez's biography

The late show contains years where Fez experienced genuine documented difficulty participating on-air.

Without older material, a listener can mistakenly infer that Fez was always essentially a quiet sidekick whom Ron pulled along.

Nature Boy contradicts that model.

When the persona was working, Fez could:

- take over a segment;
- improvise rapidly;
- sustain character logic;
- respond to live room material;
- control pacing and vocal intensity;
- make Ron function more like delighted audience/foil than rescuer.

This is why Nature Boy should be used as **biographical evidence**, not merely filed under wrestling impressions.

---

## Relationship to R&F wrestling culture

Nature Boy sits inside the show's broader use of professional-wrestling grammar:

- Big ASS Night of Fights;
- Al Dukes' exit framing;
- worked/shoot ambiguity;
- championships and fan factions;
- East Side Dave / Midnight Rider;
- Big ASS Bash;
- character competitions;
- use of wrestling promos as a model for heightened radio identity.

Nature Boy is the clearest example of Fez directly using **promo language as comedy performance**.
