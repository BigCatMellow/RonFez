# Characters and Performance Personas

This directory is for recurring constructed characters, impersonations, performance personas, and theatrical devices in the Ron & Fez universe.

It is deliberately separate from `people/` because a performer and the character they perform are not the same historical object.

## Active case files

- [`nature-boy.md`](nature-boy.md) — Fez Whatley's Ric Flair-inspired signature performance mode, spanning Florida/Ron & Ron roots through WNEW, XM, the Dave/Fez Character Showdown and the 2010 Nature Brothers performance.

## Required fields

Each character file should eventually establish:

- performer;
- first known appearance;
- first securely surviving appearance;
- original premise;
- recurring language/catchphrases;
- music/sound cues;
- whether the audience was expected to know it was fictional;
- evolution across eras;
- major appearances;
- relationship to the performer's real-life radio identity;
- surviving audio/video;
- later callbacks and memorial use;
- evidence grades;
- unresolved origin claims.

## Governing rule

Do not collapse a real person into a character, and do not treat a character as evidence of the performer's private psychology without independent support.

For Fez especially, characters are historically important because they often reveal performance capacity that is obscured if the late silent/struggling years are treated as his entire career.
