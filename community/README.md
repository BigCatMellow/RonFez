## Evidence rule

The fan world generated enormous amounts of rumor. Therefore:

- a contemporaneous board post proves **that the claim was being made**, not necessarily that it was true;
- later nostalgia proves **what survived in collective memory**, not hidden backstage facts;
- participant recollection proves what that participant remembers/understood, not what every person knew;
- exact identities and private relationships are included only where publicly established by participants or reliable records;
- modern people-search/data-broker sites are not used to deanonymize old handles;
- a restored RFNet gallery date is **not automatically an event date**;
- a current participant who corrects their own date from files is evidence that documentary records should outrank unaided memory.
