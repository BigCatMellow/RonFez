## Major governance breakthrough

A contemporary **October 11, 2002** post by FoundryMusicJeff says, in substance:

- **“how I run things at Ronfez.Net”**;
- calls RFNet **“my board”**;
- describes his moderation rules;
- refers to OA.com's use of **“my board software.”**

A later GVac memorial entry is signed **FoundryMusicJeff Shain** and says Jeff and GVac were part of RFNet's early days.

The safe current conclusion is therefore:

> **Jeff Shain / FoundryMusicJeff was a principal early operator of RonFez.net, with direct governance authority and technical/software authority by 2002.**

This does not yet prove he was the sole founder/domain owner on launch day. The remaining question is the exact division of founding, ownership, software, administration and content roles among Jeff, JustJon, MojoPin and later admins.

## Evidence rule

The archive separates:

- what directly survives;
- what a contemporary fan said;
- what a participant later remembers;
- what later fans collectively remember;
- what remains unknown.

A board post proves a statement was made, not that every allegation was true. A participant is authoritative about their own experience, not everybody else's motives. A restored gallery date is not automatically an event date. Modern data-broker searches are not used to deanonymize old handles.
