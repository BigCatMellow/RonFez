# Board Gossip — episode-by-episode forensic ledger

## Purpose

This file will become a complete index of **East Side Dave's Board Gossip** segments.

The goal is not merely to list dates. For every segment, the archive should identify:

- source board(s);
- people/handles mentioned;
- original thread/post if recoverable;
- Dave's framing;
- music/transition cues;
- Ron/Fez/Earl/Chris reactions;
- whether the subject calls in or appears later;
- whether the board discussion changes because of the radio attention;
- whether the story is primarily R&F, O&A, Paltalk, staff or civilian drama.

This is the most direct way to reconstruct **the off-air internet show that fed the on-air show**.

---

# Evidence notation

- **A** — original full broadcast / publisher episode metadata
- **B** — strong contemporary corroboration
- **C** — later participant account
- **D** — contemporary fan archive/thread
- **E** — later fan memory

# Research methods

1. Search full-show archives by date around known Board Gossip compilations.
2. Listen for theme music and board-name transitions.
3. Search surviving RFNet/Wackbag pages for distinctive phrases from audio.
4. Compare timestamp/post time to determine whether Dave was reporting same-day or prior-day material.
5. Identify repeated people across multiple boards.
6. Record deleted/missing original threads explicitly rather than inventing content.
7. Where the original post is gone, use quotes only if preserved in audio or another contemporaneous archive.

# Why this ledger matters

A complete Board Gossip database could eventually let us quantify questions that are currently just impressions:

- Which board supplied the most stories?
- How many Board Gossip stories were actually about R&F versus O&A?
- Which civilian personalities received the most airtime?
- Did radio attention prolong board fights?
- Did some users deliberately post to get on Board Gossip?
- How quickly could an off-air post become national satellite content?
- When did Paltalk replace/static-board stories, if at all?
- When did the friends-of-show ecology begin to decline?

That would turn one of the show's most ephemeral recurring bits into a searchable map of its **civilian information system**.
