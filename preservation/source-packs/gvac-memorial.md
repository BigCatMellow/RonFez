# Source pack — GVac / Greg Petraitis memorial guestbook

## Why this source is preservation-critical

The 2016 GVac memorial guestbook is one of the densest surviving first-person records of the early Ron & Fez civilian community.

It preserves memories from people who knew GVac through:

- Ron & Fez;
- RonFez.net;
- early bar crawls/events;
- long-term personal friendships;
- Weird Medicine;
- the broader radio/community ecosystem.

If the page disappears, it would not merely remove a biography of one person. It would erase dozens of small first-person fragments about how the early community actually functioned.

**Visible signer**

This entry should be cross-preserved with:
- `../../community/12-rfnet-governance-foundrymusicjeff.md`

Evidence grade:
- **C/B participant retrospective**.

- nevertheless felt they knew him as a friend through years of radio/podcast listening.

# Preservation risks

The memorial is hosted on a personal/specialty website rather than a large institutional archive.

Risks include:

- site closure;
- CMS/plugin failure causing comments to disappear;
- database loss;
- embedded YouTube links dying;
- formatting changes making names/dates difficult to recover;
- partial Wayback captures that omit dynamically loaded comments.

Because the memorial's **comments are the historical evidence**, preserving only the top page title is not enough.

---

# Required preservation actions

1. Keep the live URL in the repository-wide manifest.
2. Maintain a Wayback snapshot where comments are actually visible.
3. Preserve the reported **171-entry count** as a completeness benchmark.
4. Preserve names/dates of historically material entries in this source pack.
5. Preserve stable IDs from outbound media links such as `QPKzN7YjY1w`.
6. Cross-reference claims into person/event/community files rather than relying on this page as the only explanation.
7. If the page ever goes offline, compare recovered copies against the 171-entry benchmark before calling the recovery complete.

---

# What this pack deliberately does not do

It does **not** reproduce all 171 memorial messages into this GitHub repository.

Those entries remain copyrighted expressions by their individual authors. For historical preservation this project retains:

- the source locator;
- archive locator when available;
- identity/date metadata;
- factual claims materially used by the project;
- only small excerpts/phrases where needed to identify evidence.

A full authorized archival export would be preferable if Doctor Steve/the site owner ever makes one available for preservation.

# Core historical value

The GVac guestbook is one of the project's best examples of why **oral/community history cannot be reconstructed from show audio alone**.

In one page it preserves:

- GVac's public identity;
- early RFNet origin testimony;
- FoundryMusicJeff's identity;
- 2000/01–2001 community memories;
- bar crawls;
- softball;
- long-term friendships;
- the transition from listener to recurring radio contributor;
- the emotional reality of people who knew the civilian cast only through audio.

It should remain a P0 preservation target.
