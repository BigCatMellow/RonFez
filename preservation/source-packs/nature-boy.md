# Source pack — Fez Whatley's Nature Boy

## Preservation purpose

This file preserves the **evidence chain** for Fez Whatley's Ric Flair-inspired Nature Boy performance so that a dead webpage does not erase the chronology.

It is not a transcript collection. For third-party copyrighted pages, this pack records metadata, the historical fact supplied by the source, stable identifiers and only the minimum excerpt needed to identify the evidence.

**Source**

**Why it matters**

The Friday program is explicitly described as reaching back to the **Tampa / Ron & Ron** material and includes a segment labeled:

> `Fez is The Nature Boy: Marge Schott`

This is the strongest surviving published bridge between Nature Boy and the Florida Ron & Ron era.

**What it establishes**
- Nature Boy existed before WNEW.
- A Ron & Ron-era Nature Boy performance targeted/discussed Marge Schott.
- SiriusXM/R&F themselves considered the clip part of the Florida retrospective corpus.

**What it does not establish**
- original broadcast date;
- that this was the first Nature Boy performance;
- exact segment duration;
- exact music bed.

**Preservation priority:** highest.

## NB-02 — contemporary time window supplied by Marge Schott history

### December 1992 national scandal

**URL**
- https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/opinions/1992/12/11/whats-fit-for-marge-schott/7fb5f74e-093d-4db2-96a0-bc5c333eaaed/

**Source**
- Washington Post, December 10/11, 1992

**What it establishes**
- Schott's racist remarks and related controversy were already national news in December 1992.

### February 3, 1993 MLB suspension

**URL**
- https://pryorcenter.uark.edu/projects/KATV/1-person-topic/Person-RYP-SHE.pdf

**Source**
- archival television-news index documenting February 3, 1993 coverage of Schott's possible/scheduled suspension.

**What it establishes**
- February 1993 is another hard contemporary point in the first major Schott controversy.

### 1996 renewed controversy

**URLs**
- https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/opinions/1996/05/08/why-is-marge-schott-in-baseball/a1f4d794-4a08-423f-9d25-91fb3a98cf16/
- https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1996-05-09-sp-2217-story.html

**What they establish**
- Schott became major national news again in May 1996 after remarks about Hitler.

### Chronology consequence

Because the surviving R&F retrospective title only says `Nature Boy: Marge Schott`, **the underlying clip cannot yet be assigned automatically to 1992–93 rather than 1996**.

However, the clip must fall within the Ron & Ron era and is likely tied to one of these national Schott-news cycles.

Current safe bound:

> **Nature Boy is definitely a Ron & Ron-era character; the Marge Schott clip most plausibly dates to the 1992–93 or 1996 Schott controversy windows. Exact first use remains unresolved.**

This replaces any stronger unsupported statement that the debut was definitively late 1992 or early 1993.

## Current earliest-use verdict

### Established

- Nature Boy existed during **The Ron & Ron Show**.
- It was sufficiently established to leave a Florida-era clip later labeled `Fez is The Nature Boy: Marge Schott`.
- a Florida radio-industry listener remembers smoke filling the studio as part of the bit's production ritual.

### Narrowed but unresolved

The Marge Schott title creates two historically plausible contemporary windows:

1. **late 1992–1993** — first major national Schott racism controversy and MLB sanction;
2. **May–June 1996** — renewed Schott controversy and suspension.

No surviving source inspected so far identifies which news cycle produced the archived Nature Boy clip.

### Not yet established

- first-ever Nature Boy date;
- whether the Marge Schott segment was the debut;
- who proposed the impression;
- original music cue;
- earliest surviving audio filename.

**Current verdict:**

> **ORIGIN CONFIRMED TO RON & RON; FIRST PERFORMANCE STILL UNRESOLVED. EARLIEST DATEABLE CLUE IS THE MARGE SCHOTT CLIP, PROBABLY FROM EITHER THE 1992–93 OR 1996 CONTROVERSY WINDOW.**
